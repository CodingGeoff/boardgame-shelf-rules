## Overview

Flip fruit cards; when exactly five of one fruit is visible, ring the bell first.

## Setup

1. Bell in the centre; deal all cards face down.

## How to play

1. Flip your top card outward on your pile.
2. Only top cards count.
3. Exactly 5 of one fruit: ring the bell and collect all face-up cards.
4. Wrong ring: give 1 card to each player.

## Game end & scoring

- Players out of cards are eliminated; last player wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Players out of cards are eliminated; last player wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Flip your top card outward on your pile.
3. **Setup check:** Bell in the centre; deal all cards face down.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Exactly 5, not more.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Track the fruit with the highest count.
- Flip outward.
- Stay calm.

## Common mistakes

- Exactly 5, not more.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

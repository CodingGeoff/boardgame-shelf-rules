## Overview

Boxed murder mystery scripts: fixed player counts, playable once: choose roles, read scripts, search clues, discuss and vote.

## Setup

1. Choose the script and assign characters (fixed count).

## How to play

1. Read personal scripts; rounds of clue searching and discussion.

## Game end & scoring

- Vote for the culprit and reveal the truth.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Vote for the culprit and reveal the truth.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Read personal scripts; rounds of clue searching and discussion.
3. **Setup check:** Choose the script and assign characters (fixed count).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Each script can only be played once.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- The organiser reads rules first.
- Gather the exact player count.
- Role-play your character.

## Common mistakes

- Each script can only be played once.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** when time is short; anyone who has already played the same script

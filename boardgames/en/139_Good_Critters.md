## Overview

Animal gang splitting loot: the boss divides, everyone secretly votes, sneaks, robs or guards; 'The Fuzz!' ends the game.

## Setup

1. Shuffle loot and put The Fuzz! randomly in the bottom half.
2. Each player takes action cards (Aye, Nay, Sneak, Guard, Rob) and threat tokens; the host is the first boss.

## How to play

1. Reveal players + 2 loot cards; the boss divides them freely (may exclude people) but must distribute all.
2. Everyone secretly plays 1 action card; resolve from the boss in seat order.
3. Sneak: take the top card of the deck (only the first sneak in order succeeds). Rob: take a random loot card from a player you threatened. Guard: anyone robbing you loses a card to you.
4. More ayes: the split stands and the boss stays. More nays: the split is void; the first nay voter clockwise from the boss becomes boss, 2 more cards are added and redivided.

## Game end & scoring

- When The Fuzz! is revealed, highest loot value wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When The Fuzz! is revealed, highest loot value wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Reveal players + 2 loot cards; the boss divides them freely (may exclude people) but must distribute all.
3. **Setup check:** Shuffle loot and put The Fuzz! randomly in the bottom half.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Only the first sneak succeeds each round.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Give enough to pass votes.
- Threaten before robbing.
- Guard counters robbers.

## Common mistakes

- Only the first sneak succeeds each round.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

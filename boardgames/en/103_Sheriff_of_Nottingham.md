## Overview

Smuggle goods into the city: bag goods, declare a legal type and count; the sheriff inspects or lets you pass — bribes welcome.

## Setup

1. Deal goods; rotate sheriff.

## How to play

1. Market: exchange cards.
2. Load up to 5 cards and declare one legal type with the true count.
3. The sheriff inspects or passes; lies are fined; honest merchants are compensated.

## Game end & scoring

- After everyone has been sheriff (set times), score goods, coins, king/queen bonuses.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After everyone has been sheriff (set times), score goods, coins, king/queen bonuses.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Market: exchange cards.
3. **Setup check:** Deal goods; rotate sheriff.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The count must be true; the type may be a lie.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Be honest sometimes.
- Bribe with goods and promises.
- Legal majorities matter.

## Common mistakes

- The count must be true; the type may be a lie.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

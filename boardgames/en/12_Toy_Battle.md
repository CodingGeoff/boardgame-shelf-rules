## Overview

Two toy factions fight over a carpet: each turn either draw 2 tiles or deploy 1, aiming for medals or the enemy HQ.

## Setup

1. Place the board; each player claims an HQ.
2. Shuffle your toy tiles face down as a supply; draw starting tiles to your rack.

## How to play

1. Choose one: Refill — draw 2 tiles (rack limit 8); or Deploy — place 1 tile connected to your HQ through your own spaces.
2. You may deploy onto an empty space, onto your own tile (replacing it), or onto an enemy tile of strictly lower strength.
3. Surround or connect medal areas to collect medals.

## Game end & scoring

- Capture the enemy HQ for an instant win, or reach the medal target first.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Capture the enemy HQ for an instant win, or reach the medal target first.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Choose one: Refill — draw 2 tiles (rack limit 8); or Deploy — place 1 tile connected to your HQ through your own spaces.
3. **Setup check:** Place the board; each player claims an HQ.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Overwriting needs strictly higher strength.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Balance drawing and deploying — too much drawing loses map control.
- Keep high-strength toys to capture key spaces or guard your HQ entrance.
- Cut opponents' connection to their HQ so their front line cannot be reinforced.

## Common mistakes

- Overwriting needs strictly higher strength.
- Deployment must connect to your HQ through your tiles.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

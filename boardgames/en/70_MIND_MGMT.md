## Overview

One recruiter moves secretly and recruits; rogue agents ask about locations to pin down his position.

## Setup

1. The recruiter secretly selects recruitment conditions and starting position.

## How to play

1. The recruiter moves secretly and recruits matching people.
2. Rogue agents move and ask location traits; the recruiter must answer truthfully.
3. Agents have special abilities and 3 direct accusations.

## Game end & scoring

- Recruiter wins at 12 recruits or by surviving; agents win by locating him.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Recruiter wins at 12 recruits or by surviving; agents win by locating him.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The recruiter moves secretly and recruits matching people.
3. **Setup check:** The recruiter secretly selects recruitment conditions and starting position.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The recruiter must answer truthfully.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Agents spread out to narrow intersections.
- The recruiter uses detours.
- Combine agent abilities.

## Common mistakes

- The recruiter must answer truthfully.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

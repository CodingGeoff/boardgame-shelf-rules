## Overview

Classic werewolf: night kills and powers, day discussion and votes; villagers must eliminate all wolves.

## Setup

1. A moderator distributes balanced roles.

## How to play

1. Night: werewolves kill, special roles act.
2. Day: discuss and vote to eliminate.

## Game end & scoring

- Villagers win when all wolves are gone; wolves win at parity.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Villagers win when all wolves are gone; wolves win at parity.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Night: werewolves kill, special roles act.
3. **Setup check:** A moderator distributes balanced roles.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Eliminated players stay silent.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Keep logical consistency.
- Seers time reveals.
- Wolves split their votes.

## Common mistakes

- Eliminated players stay silent.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

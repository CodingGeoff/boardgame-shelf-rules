## Overview

Play cards simultaneously and add them to rows; whoever places the 6th card in a row takes the bullheads.

## Setup

1. Deal 10 cards each; start 4 rows with 1 card each.
2. Bullheads: most cards 1; ending in 5 = 2; multiples of 10 = 3; multiples of 11 = 5; 55 = 7.

## How to play

1. Everyone chooses a card simultaneously and reveals.
2. From lowest to highest, place each card at the end of the row whose last card is lower and closest.
3. Placing the 6th card: take the first 5; your card starts the row.
4. Card lower than every row end: choose any row to take.

## Game end & scoring

- Play 10 turns per hand; continue hands until someone reaches 66 points; fewest bullheads wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Play 10 turns per hand; continue hands until someone reaches 66 points; fewest bullheads wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Everyone chooses a card simultaneously and reveals.
3. **Setup check:** Deal 10 cards each; start 4 rows with 1 card each.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Always place in the row with the smallest difference.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Mid-range numbers are the most dangerous — play them early.
- Low cards can take a cheap row to gain control.
- Track played numbers.

## Common mistakes

- Always place in the row with the smallest difference.
- Fewest points wins.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 30–45 min; budget about 55 min for a first game.
- Total: about 60 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Everyone has 10 cards. Simultaneously choose a card; resolve lowest first, placing each after the row whose end is closest below it. The 6th card in a row takes the 5 before it. A card lower than all row ends must take a row of the player's choice. Fewest bull heads wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Play middle numbers early; hold extremes.
- **Mid:** Count rows — avoid rows with 5 cards.
- **Late:** With a very low card, take the cheapest row.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: After taking a row, where does my card go?**  
  A: It starts the new row.
- **Q: When does it end?**  
  A: Commonly when someone reaches 66 bull heads.

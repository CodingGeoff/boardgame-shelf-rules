## Overview

Splendor with Pokémon: collect Poké Balls, catch Pokémon and evolve them into stronger forms.

## Setup

1. Lay out Pokémon cards in tiers plus rare/legendary cards; set up ball tokens by player count.

## How to play

1. Take balls, reserve a Pokémon, or catch (buy) a Pokémon as in Splendor.
2. At the end of your turn, you may evolve one Pokémon by meeting its evolution requirement.

## Game end & scoring

- Reach the point target (see rulebook) and finish the round; also consider the number of Pokémon types.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Reach the point target (see rulebook) and finish the round; also consider the number of Pokémon types.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Take balls, reserve a Pokémon, or catch (buy) a Pokémon as in Splendor.
3. **Setup check:** Lay out Pokémon cards in tiers plus rare/legendary cards; set up ball tokens by player count.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Only one evolution per turn.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Plan evolution lines — evolving saves actions.
- Master Balls act as wild.
- Watch which legendary cards are approachable.

## Common mistakes

- Only one evolution per turn.
- Token limit applies.

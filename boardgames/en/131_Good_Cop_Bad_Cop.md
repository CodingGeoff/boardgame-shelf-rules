## Overview

Hidden-faction shootout: three integrity cards decide whether you are honest or crooked; investigate, arm and shoot the opposing leader (Agent or Kingpin).

## Setup

1. Remove guns and cards whose top-right number exceeds the player count.
2. Shuffle the Agent and Kingpin with some integrity cards and deal 1 each, then deal 2 more; arrange face down — order never changes.
3. Deal 1 equipment card each; guns in the centre.

## How to play

1. Honest: majority honest and no Kingpin, or holding the Agent. Crooked: majority crooked and no Agent, or holding the Kingpin.
2. Choose one action: Investigate (peek at one face-down integrity card of anyone); Equip (draw equipment, then reveal one of yours); Arm (take a gun, then reveal one of yours); Shoot (shoot your target, return the gun).
3. Shot players reveal all integrity cards; normal cops die; the Agent or Kingpin takes a wound and draws equipment on the first hit and dies on the second.
4. Equipment is usable only when you hold exactly 1; it may be played after an action is announced (steal guns, disarm, swap cards, revive…).

## Game end & scoring

- Kingpin killed: honest side wins. Agent killed: crooked side wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Kingpin killed: honest side wins. Agent killed: crooked side wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Honest: majority honest and no Kingpin, or holding the Agent. Crooked: majority crooked and no Agent, or holding the Kingpin.
3. **Setup check:** Remove guns and cards whose top-right number exceeds the player count.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Integrity card order is fixed.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Cross-check investigations.
- Aiming reveals intentions.
- Leaders survive the first shot.

## Common mistakes

- Integrity card order is fixed.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

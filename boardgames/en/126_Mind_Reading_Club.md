## Overview

A party game of writing matching answers: the asker poses a question with 10+ answers; everyone writes 6 different answers and flips them to find matches, or bets on how many matches occur.

## Setup

1. Each player: 4 pawns, 6 writing boards, a marker; 150 in chips; board and prize bowl in the centre with 30.
2. Set the round bonuses per the reference card.

## How to play

1. Ask: the asker poses a question with at least 10 answers (e.g. letters of the alphabet).
2. Answer: everyone, including the asker, writes 6 different answers secretly.
3. Act clockwise from the asker's left: reveal one of your answers — anyone with the same or very similar answer must reveal it, you win the whole bowl and the glass pawn advances per matching group; or pay 10 to predict where the glass pawn will finish (max 2 pawns per number).
4. A round ends when someone has revealed all 6 answers.

## Game end & scoring

- The asker checks unrevealed answers; each invalid answer costs 20.
- Pay out predictions; richest after the rounds wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** The asker checks unrevealed answers; each invalid answer costs 20.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Ask: the asker poses a question with at least 10 answers (e.g. letters of the alphabet).
3. **Setup check:** Each player: 4 pawns, 6 writing boards, a marker; 150 in chips; board and prize bowl in the centre with 30.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Questions need 10+ answers; no looking things up.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Common answers match more.
- Bet once you see how fast matches occur.
- Ask broad questions.

## Common mistakes

- Questions need 10+ answers; no looking things up.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 20–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 40 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> A question with many answers; everyone writes one, then reveals and bets on matches. Score for thinking alike.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Write common answers.
- **Mid:** Read others' thinking.
- **Late:** Bet on consensus.

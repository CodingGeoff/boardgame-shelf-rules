## Overview

Antique traders bluff authenticity while buying, exhibiting, trading and auctioning; best collection wins.

## Setup

1. See the rulebook for setup.

## How to play

1. Phases: purchase, exhibit, trade, auction; collectibles may be genuine or fake.
2. Free trading between players.

## Game end & scoring

- Collection value per your goals; highest wins (see rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Collection value per your goals; highest wins (see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Phases: purchase, exhibit, trade, auction; collectibles may be genuine or fake.
3. **Setup check:** See the rulebook for setup.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Crowdfunded edition — follow the rulebook.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Mix genuine and fakes.
- Bid up rivals' targets.
- Appraise high-value lots.

## Common mistakes

- Crowdfunded edition — follow the rulebook.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

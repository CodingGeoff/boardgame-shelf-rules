## Overview

Antique traders bluff authenticity while buying, exhibiting, trading and auctioning; best collection wins.

## Setup

1. See the rulebook for setup.

## How to play

1. Phases: purchase, exhibit, trade, auction; collectibles may be genuine or fake.
2. Free trading between players.

## Game end & scoring

- Collection value per your goals; highest wins (see rulebook).

- Bid up rivals' targets.

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 45–60 min; budget about 70 min for a first game.
- Total: about 80 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Antique dealers buy, sell, trade and auction items that may be real or fake. Better collections score more. Highest value wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Buy early to learn the fake distribution.
- **Mid:** Exploit information gaps.
- **Late:** Assemble the best sets.

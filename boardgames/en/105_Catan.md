## Overview

Roll for wood, brick, sheep, wheat and ore; build roads, settlements and cities; first to 10 points.

## Setup

1. Build the map (6 and 8 not adjacent); snake-place two settlements and roads.

## How to play

1. Roll for production.
2. Trade with players or the bank (4:1, ports).
3. Build roads, settlements, cities, development cards.
4. On a 7, discard half if over 7 cards and move the robber.

## Game end & scoring

- First to 10 VP on their turn wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to 10 VP on their turn wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Roll for production.
3. **Setup check:** Build the map (6 and 8 not adjacent); snake-place two settlements and roads.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Settlements must be 2 edges apart.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Opening spots: probability and diversity.
- Ports convert surplus.
- Do not trade with the leader.

## Common mistakes

- Settlements must be 2 edges apart.

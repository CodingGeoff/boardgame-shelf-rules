## Overview

Roll for wood, brick, sheep, wheat and ore; build roads, settlements and cities; first to 10 points.

## Setup

1. Build the map (6 and 8 not adjacent); snake-place two settlements and roads.

## How to play

1. Roll for production.
2. Trade with players or the bank (4:1, ports).
3. Build roads, settlements, cities, development cards.
4. On a 7, discard half if over 7 cards and move the robber.

## Game end & scoring

- First to 10 VP on their turn wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to 10 VP on their turn wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Roll for production.
3. **Setup check:** Build the map (6 and 8 not adjacent); snake-place two settlements and roads.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Settlements must be 2 edges apart.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Opening spots: probability and diversity.
- Ports convert surplus.
- Do not trade with the leader.

## Common mistakes

- Settlements must be 2 edges apart.

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 60–90 min; budget about 110 min for a first game.
- Total: about 120 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Rolls produce wood, brick, sheep, wheat, ore. Build roads, settlements, cities, buy development cards. Trade with players, bank 4:1, ports 3:1/2:1. A 7 moves the robber. Settlement 1, city 2, longest road/largest army 2 each; first to 10 wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Pick diverse numbers; 6 and 8 are best.
- **Mid:** Trade for what you lack.
- **Late:** Grab longest road or largest army.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Trade with the bank?**  
  A: 4:1, ports 3:1 or 2:1.
- **Q: Over 7 cards on a 7?**  
  A: Discard half.

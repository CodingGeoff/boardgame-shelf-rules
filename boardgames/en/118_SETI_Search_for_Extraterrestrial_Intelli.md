## Overview

Run a research institute: launch probes, scan stars, analyse data and find alien life on a rotating solar system.

## Setup

1. Each player: 4 publicity, 4 credits, 3 energy, 5 cards, income card.

## How to play

1. Main actions: launch, move, orbit or land, scan, analyse, play cards, etc.; free actions too.
2. The solar system rotates, changing distances.
3. Alien species are revealed as traces accumulate.

## Game end & scoring

- After the final round, score tiles, missions and species; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After the final round, score tiles, missions and species; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Main actions: launch, move, orbit or land, scan, analyse, play cards, etc.; free actions too.
3. **Setup check:** Each player: 4 publicity, 4 credits, 3 energy, 5 cards, income card.
4. **The one rule people forget:** First games can exceed 2 hours.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Data and analysis lead to aliens.
- Time launches with rotation.
- Income choices shape the mid-game.

## Common mistakes

- First games can exceed 2 hours.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** experienced gamers who want a deep think
- **Not ideal for:** relaxed chatting sessions

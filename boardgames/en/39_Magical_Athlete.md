## Overview

Dozens of racers with unique powers run 4 races: snake-draft your racers, roll and race; the race ends when second place crosses.

## Setup

1. Reveal 2 × players racer cards and snake-draft (1→N, N→1).

## How to play

1. Before each race, everyone secretly picks an unused racer.
2. Roll and move; resolve powers.
3. First and second place score; flip the track between races (easy vs hazard track).

## Game end & scoring

- After 4 races, highest total wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 4 races, highest total wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Before each race, everyone secretly picks an unused racer.
3. **Setup check:** Reveal 2 × players racer cards and snake-draft (1→N, N→1).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Moving 0 spaces does not count as moving.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Draft synergies or counters to popular racers.
- 'CAN' powers are optional; others are mandatory.
- Counter-pick in the last race.

## Common mistakes

- Moving 0 spaces does not count as moving.
- The race ends when the second racer finishes.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** families with young children
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

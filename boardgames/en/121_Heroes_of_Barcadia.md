## Overview

Dungeon crawling plus drinking: the drink in your cup is your health; take damage, take a sip; empty cup and you are out.

## Setup

1. Each hero has a cup filled with a drink; build the dungeon start.

## How to play

1. Explore tiles, fight monsters and traps; damage means drinking.

## Game end & scoring

- Win per the rulebook (e.g. last standing or objective).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win per the rulebook (e.g. last standing or objective).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Explore tiles, fight monsters and traps; damage means drinking.
3. **Setup check:** Each hero has a cup filled with a drink; build the dungeon start.
4. **The one rule people forget:** 21+ in the original listing; drink responsibly.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Non-alcoholic drinks work too.
- It is about the party.
- Agree amounts first.

## Common mistakes

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Tiles face down; fill your cup (any drink works).
2. I explore a room: a monster.
3. I roll the d20, fail and take damage — one sip.
4. I win the next fight and draw loot.
5. Defeat the boss to win; an empty cup eliminates you.

## Overview

Four colours of polyominoes: your pieces may only touch corner to corner; fewest leftover squares wins.

## Setup

1. Each colour has 21 pieces; play order blue, yellow, red, green.

## How to play

1. Your first piece covers your corner.
2. Each later piece touches your colour at a corner only (never edge to edge).
3. If you cannot place, pass.

## Game end & scoring

- Count leftover squares; bonuses for placing all pieces (+15, +20 if the last is the monomino).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Count leftover squares; bonuses for placing all pieces (+15, +20 if the last is the monomino).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Your first piece covers your corner.
3. **Setup check:** Each colour has 21 pieces; play order blue, yellow, red, green.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Your pieces may touch other colours along edges.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Big awkward pieces first.
- Block opponents' corners.
- Save the monomino.

## Common mistakes

- Your pieces may touch other colours along edges.

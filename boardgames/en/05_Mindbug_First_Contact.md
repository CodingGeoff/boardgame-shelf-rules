## Overview

A lightweight duel card game: play creatures to attack, but each player has two Mindbugs to steal a creature the moment the opponent plays it.

## Setup

1. Each player starts with 3 life, 5 cards in hand, a 5-card draw pile and 2 Mindbug cards.

## How to play

1. On your turn either play a creature or attack with one creature.
2. When you play a creature, your opponent may use a Mindbug to take control of it (and you take another turn).
3. When attacking, the defender may block with a creature; higher power wins, ties destroy both.
4. Unblocked attacks cost the defender 1 life. Draw back to 5 cards while your draw pile lasts.

## Game end & scoring

- Reduce your opponent to 0 life, or win if the opponent cannot act.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Reduce your opponent to 0 life, or win if the opponent cannot act.
2. **What you do on a turn:** On your turn either play a creature or attack with one creature.
3. **Setup check:** Each player starts with 3 life, 5 cards in hand, a 5-card draw pile and 2 Mindbug cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Using a Mindbug gives the opponent an extra turn.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Bait your opponent's Mindbugs with strong but not crucial creatures.
- Track how many Mindbugs each side has left.
- Keywords (Poisonous, Tough, Frenzy, Sneaky, Hunter) decide most fights.

## Common mistakes

- Using a Mindbug gives the opponent an extra turn.
- Poisonous kills any creature it battles.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

2. I attack with a power-5 creature; he blocks with a power-6 one and mine dies.

### Rules FAQ / rulings

- **Q: Can Mindbugs be reused?**  
  A: No — you have 2, each used once.
- **Q: Who gets a stolen creature's 'play' effect?**  
  A: The player who stole it.

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 15–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 35 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Each player has a 10-card deck, 5 in hand and 3 life. On your turn play a creature or attack with one. The defender may block — higher power survives; unblocked deals 1 damage. Twist: each player has 2 Mindbugs — when the opponent plays a creature you may take it for yourself. Reduce the opponent to 0 life.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Lead with mid-power creatures to bait the opponent's Mindbugs.
- **Mid:** Save your bombs until their Mindbugs are gone; master Poisonous, Tough, Frenzy, Hunter.
- **Late:** Count life: at 1 life a Hunter or Frenzy creature can close it out.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: After my creature is Mindbugged, do I get another turn?**  
  A: Yes, you take another turn (per rulebook).
- **Q: What if my deck runs out?**  
  A: Play on from hand; a player who can't act loses (see rulebook).

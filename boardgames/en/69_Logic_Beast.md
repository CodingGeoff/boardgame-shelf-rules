## Overview

Situation puzzles: a host reads the riddle; players ask yes/no questions to reconstruct the bizarre truth.

## Setup

1. Choose a host who draws a story card.

## How to play

1. The host reads the riddle side only.
2. Players ask questions answered with yes, no or irrelevant.
3. Someone states the key truth to solve.

## Game end & scoring

- Solve the mystery; optionally score by questions or time.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Solve the mystery; optionally score by questions or time.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The host reads the riddle side only.
3. **Setup check:** Choose a host who draws a story card.
4. **The one rule people forget:** 16+ content; some answers are dark.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Start with broad questions.
- Record known facts.
- The host may guide when stuck.

## Common mistakes

- 16+ content; some answers are dark.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; friends who prefer working together; people who love deduction and guessing identities; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

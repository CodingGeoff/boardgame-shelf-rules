## Overview

Cheating is allowed: moth cards can only be secretly dumped, but the guard bug catches cheaters.

## Setup

1. The guard card goes to the oldest player; deal 8 cards each; start the discard with a number card.

## How to play

1. Play a card exactly 1 higher or lower than the top (5 and 1 connect).
2. Bug cards: Ant (others draw), Spider (give a non-moth card), Mosquito (slap; slowest takes cards), Cockroach (race to play same number).
3. Moths cannot be played normally — cheat them away under the table, in a sleeve, etc.
4. If you cannot play, draw 1.

## Game end & scoring

- Caught cheaters take back the card plus penalties; first to empty their hand wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Caught cheaters take back the card plus penalties; first to empty their hand wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play a card exactly 1 higher or lower than the top (5 and 1 connect).
3. **Setup check:** The guard card goes to the oldest player; deal 8 cards each; start the discard with a number card.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The guard cannot cheat but may play moths.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Dump moths during chaotic moments.
- One card at a time.
- As guard, watch hands that shrink suddenly.

## Common mistakes

- The guard cannot cheat but may play moths.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

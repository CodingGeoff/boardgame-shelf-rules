## Overview

See others' spell stones but not your own: announce spells to cast; wrong guesses hurt you.

## Setup

1. Distribute spell stones (1–8) face outward so you cannot see yours.

## How to play

1. Announce a spell number; if you have it, it takes effect; otherwise lose life by die roll.
2. Each subsequent spell in a turn must be equal or higher.

## Game end & scoring

- A round ends when someone casts all their stones or reduces an opponent to 0 life; score on the tower (winner and survivors differ, see rulebook).
- First to 8 points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** A round ends when someone casts all their stones or reduces an opponent to 0 life; score on the tower (winner and survivors differ, see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Announce a spell number; if you have it, it takes effect; otherwise lose life by die roll.
3. **Setup check:** Distribute spell stones (1–8) face outward so you cannot see yours.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Spell numbers cannot decrease within a turn.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Count visible spells to deduce yours.
- Try low numbers first.
- The owl (8) lets you peek at secret stones.

## Common mistakes

- Spell numbers cannot decrease within a turn.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

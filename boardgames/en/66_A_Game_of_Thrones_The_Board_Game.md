## Overview

Westeros conquest: secret orders, bidding on influence tracks, betrayal and alliances; first to 7 castles wins.

## Setup

1. Each house sets up per its sheet; tracks and supply set; Westeros decks shuffled.

## How to play

1. Westeros phase: draw events (mustering, supply, wildlings).
2. Planning: place orders face down (march, support, defend, raid, consolidate power).
3. Action: resolve raids, marches with combat (strength + support + house card), then consolidate.

## Game end & scoring

- Control 7 castles/strongholds to win instantly; otherwise most after round 10 (tiebreaks per rules).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Control 7 castles/strongholds to win instantly; otherwise most after round 10 (tiebreaks per rules).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Westeros phase: draw events (mustering, supply, wildlings).
3. **Setup check:** Each house sets up per its sheet; tracks and supply set; Westeros decks shuffled.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Supply limits army sizes.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Support is your diplomatic currency.
- Compete on all three tracks.
- House cards return only when all are used.

## Common mistakes

- Supply limits army sizes.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; experienced gamers who want a deep think
- **Not ideal for:** relaxed chatting sessions; players who dislike conflict or backstabbing; when time is short

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 25 min (weight ●●●●).
- Play: 120–240 min; budget about 290 min for a first game.
- Total: about 315 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Play a Westeros house fighting for castles. Simultaneously place orders (march, support, defend, raid, consolidate), then resolve. Influence tracks are auctioned for turn order and powers. Win with 7 castles or the most after 10 rounds.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Secure your area and supply.
- **Mid:** Use diplomacy and guard your back.
- **Late:** Strike decisively late.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Are deals binding?**  
  A: No.
- **Q: Over supply limit?**  
  A: Disband excess units.

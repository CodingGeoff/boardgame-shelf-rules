## Overview

Zombies surround a mall: choose rooms secretly, then vote who gets thrown out when zombies break into the weakest room.

## Setup

1. Place characters (Pin-up, Tough Guy, Gunman) in rooms.

## How to play

1. Parking search, elect the chief of security, roll zombie arrivals, secretly choose moves, zombie attack votes.

## Game end & scoring

- When few survivors remain, total character values; highest wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When few survivors remain, total character values; highest wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Parking search, elect the chief of security, roll zombie arrivals, secretly choose moves, zombie attack votes.
3. **Setup check:** Place characters (Pin-up, Tough Guy, Gunman) in rooms.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Tough Guy's ability does not work in the parking lot.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Security chief has information.
- Tough Guy counts double.
- Alliances and betrayal are the fun.

## Common mistakes

- Tough Guy's ability does not work in the parking lot.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

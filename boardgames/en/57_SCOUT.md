## Overview

Ladder-climbing with a fixed hand order: play sets or runs, or scout a card from the table into your hand.

## Setup

1. Remove cards by player count and deal; you cannot rearrange, but may flip the whole hand once.

## How to play

1. Show: play adjacent cards (set or run) that beat the table set (more cards or higher).
2. Scout: take a card from either end of the table set and insert it anywhere (owner gains 1 point).
3. Scout & Show: once per round.

## Game end & scoring

- Round ends when a player empties their hand or everyone scouts in a row; score captured cards minus hand cards.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Round ends when a player empties their hand or everyone scouts in a row; score captured cards minus hand cards.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Show: play adjacent cards (set or run) that beat the table set (more cards or higher).
3. **Setup check:** Remove cards by player count and deal; you cannot rearrange, but may flip the whole hand once.
4. **The one rule people forget:** No reordering of hands.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Choose the hand orientation with more connections.
- Scouting can complete sets.
- Beaten cards become your points.

## Common mistakes

- No reordering of hands.
- Scout & Show once per round.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 15–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 35 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> You may not reorder your hand — only flip it once at the start. On your turn: Show adjacent same-number or consecutive cards to beat the current set; or Scout one card from either end of the set into anywhere in your hand (the owner gets a token); or once per round Scout & Show. Beating a set lets you collect it as points.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Decide on flipping your hand: pick the side with more adjacent sets.
- **Mid:** Scout to build long runs, but each scout gives a point away.
- **Late:** End the round when you can — each card left in hand is −1.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: When does a round end?**  
  A: When someone empties their hand, or when a Show goes unbeaten as everyone else Scouts.
- **Q: Does the player whose set went unbeaten lose points for their hand?**  
  A: No.

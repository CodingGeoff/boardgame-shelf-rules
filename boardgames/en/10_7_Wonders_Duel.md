## Overview

Two-player 7 Wonders: draft cards from a pyramid layout over 3 ages and win by science, military or points.

## Setup

1. Draft 4 wonders each (in two rounds).
2. Lay out the Age I card structure (some face down) and place the conflict pawn in the centre.

## How to play

1. Take an accessible card (not covered) and either build it, discard it for coins, or use it to build a wonder.
2. Pay resource costs; missing resources cost 2 coins + the number of that resource the opponent produces.
3. Red cards move the conflict pawn; green symbol pairs earn progress tokens.

## Game end & scoring

- Military supremacy: pawn reaches your opponent's capital — instant win.
- Scientific supremacy: 6 different science symbols — instant win.
- Otherwise, after Age III, most victory points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Military supremacy: pawn reaches your opponent's capital — instant win.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Take an accessible card (not covered) and either build it, discard it for coins, or use it to build a wonder.
3. **Setup check:** Draft 4 wonders each (in two rounds).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Only 7 wonders are built in total; the last unbuilt one is discarded.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Watch both instant-win tracks; deny cards the opponent needs.
- Chain symbols let you build later cards for free.
- Wonders that grant an extra turn are very strong.

## Common mistakes

- Only 7 wonders are built in total; the last unbuilt one is discarded.
- Taking a card can reveal cards your opponent wants.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Detailed supplement: components, worked example, scoring, FAQ, player counts, variants

### Component checklist

From the Chinese doc (original wording):

- [ ] 66 张时代卡（时代 I、II、III 各 23 张）、7 张行会卡、12 张奇迹卡
- [ ] 10 个科技（进步）标记、4 个军事掠夺标记、1 个冲突（军事）标记
- [ ] 游戏版图（军事轨道 + 科技标记区）、钱币、计分本

Before you start:
- Count the components against the rulebook list; sort cards by type — a missing card can break the balance.
- Check tokens, dice and score markers; coins or generic tokens can stand in for missing pieces.
- Hand out reference cards before the first game to save rulebook time.

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Age I: only uncovered cards in the layout can be taken. I take a lumber yard, revealing a card beneath.
2. He takes a stone pit and reveals a red military card.
3. I pay 2 coins + a resource for it; the conflict pawn moves 1 toward him.
4. I use a card to build a Wonder and gain its 'play again' effect.

### Scoring example

- Three victories: military supremacy, scientific supremacy (6 different symbols), or most points after Age III.

### Rules FAQ / rulings

- **Q: Resource cost from the bank?**  
  A: 2 coins + number of that resource the opponent produces.
- **Q: How many Wonders?**  
  A: Only 7 of 8 get built.

### Player-count differences

- 2 players.
- Best with: 2 players.

- Pantheon and Agora expansions add gods and new systems.

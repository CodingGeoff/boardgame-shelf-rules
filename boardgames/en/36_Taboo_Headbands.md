## Overview

Wear a hidden taboo word on your forehead; lure others into saying theirs while avoiding your own.

## Setup

1. Remove blank cards (or write your own).
2. Each player puts a random card on a headband facing outward.

## How to play

1. Chat freely; steer others into saying or doing their taboo naturally.
2. Do not say or do your own taboo.
3. Breaking it means taking the card's penalty; Traditional Chinese rules give 2 lives.

## Game end & scoring

- Last player still wearing a card wins (or play casually with house rules).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Last player still wearing a card wins (or play casually with house rules).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Chat freely; steer others into saying or doing their taboo naturally.
3. **Setup check:** Remove blank cards (or write your own).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Keep talking — silence is not the point.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Approach topics indirectly.
- Team up on one target.
- If everyone avoids a topic around you, it is probably yours.

## Common mistakes

- Keep talking — silence is not the point.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

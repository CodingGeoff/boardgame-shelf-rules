## Overview

Two-player idol concert duel: sing to raise Voltage, win fans from the audience, and play events with your Memory.

## Setup

1. Each player chooses 3 of 6 idols and builds an 18-card deck from them.
2. Draw 5 cards; reveal 3 fan cards in the audience.
3. Choose one idol as Centre for her skill.

## How to play

1. Song cards: raise Voltage, or spend Voltage to take a fan card from the audience.
2. Played songs become Memory, used to pay for Event cards.
3. Events interfere, draw cards or change the Centre.
4. Refill the audience after a fan is taken.

## Game end & scoring

- First to 20 fan points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to 20 fan points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Song cards: raise Voltage, or spend Voltage to take a fan card from the audience.
3. **Setup check:** Each player chooses 3 of 6 idols and builds an 18-card deck from them.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Deck construction details per rulebook.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Rotate the Centre to use the right skill at the right time.
- Core fans score high; troublesome fans have drawbacks.
- Balance Voltage building with fan-taking.

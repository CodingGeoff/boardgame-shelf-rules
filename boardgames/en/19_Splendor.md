## Overview

Take gems, buy development cards and use their permanent discounts to snowball to 15 points.

## Setup

1. Set gem tokens by player count; lay out 4 cards in each of 3 tiers and nobles (players + 1).

## How to play

1. Choose one: take 3 different gems; take 2 of the same (only if 4+ in the pile); reserve a card and take a gold; or buy a card.
2. Bought cards permanently reduce costs of that colour.
3. Nobles visit automatically when you meet their requirements.

## Game end & scoring

- When someone reaches 15 points, finish the round; highest score wins (fewest cards breaks ties).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When someone reaches 15 points, finish the round; highest score wins (fewest cards breaks ties).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Choose one: take 3 different gems; take 2 of the same (only if 4+ in the pile); reserve a card and take a gold; or buy a card.
3. **Setup check:** Set gem tokens by player count; lay out 4 cards in each of 3 tiers and nobles (players + 1).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Token limit is 10.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Early cheap cards in two colours build a fast engine.
- Reserve cards opponents need.
- Track noble requirements.

## Common mistakes

- Token limit is 10.
- Taking 2 same requires at least 4 in the pile.

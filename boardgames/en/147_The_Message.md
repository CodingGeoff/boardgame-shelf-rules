## Overview

A spy card game based on the film: intelligence cards are both hand and weapon; Lurkers, Military Intelligence and neutral roles race to collect intel.

## Setup

1. Deal identities and characters; deal hands.

## How to play

1. Draw, play cards, pass intelligence (secret, direct, document), receive.
2. Black intelligence: 3 kills you.

## Game end & scoring

- Collect 3 intel of your colour to win; neutrals vary.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Collect 3 intel of your colour to win; neutrals vary.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw, play cards, pass intelligence (secret, direct, document), receive.
3. **Setup check:** Deal identities and characters; deal hands.
4. **The one rule people forget:** 3 black intel = death.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Probes reveal identities.
- Pass black intel to enemies.
- Learn characters gradually.

## Common mistakes

- 3 black intel = death.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

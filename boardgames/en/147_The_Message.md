## Overview

A spy card game based on the film: intelligence cards are both hand and weapon; Lurkers, Military Intelligence and neutral roles race to collect intel.

## Setup

1. Deal identities and characters; deal hands.

## How to play

1. Draw, play cards, pass intelligence (secret, direct, document), receive.
2. Black intelligence: 3 kills you.

## Game end & scoring

- Collect 3 intel of your colour to win; neutrals vary.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Collect 3 intel of your colour to win; neutrals vary.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw, play cards, pass intelligence (secret, direct, document), receive.
3. **Setup check:** Deal identities and characters; deal hands.
4. **The one rule people forget:** 3 black intel = death.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Probes reveal identities.
- Pass black intel to enemies.
- Learn characters gradually.

## Common mistakes

- 3 black intel = death.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Pick a character; secret identity: Red, Blue or neutral.
2. Draw 2; play function cards.
3. Send an intel card; others accept or pass.
4. A receives a 3rd black intel and dies.
5. A Red player collects 3 red intel — Red wins.

### Scoring example

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 30–60 min; budget about 70 min for a first game.
- Total: about 85 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Espionage card game with hidden factions. Intel cards are both hand and weapon — pass intel via direct, secret or text routes; others accept or refuse, or intercept, swap and expose with action cards. Three intel of a colour fulfils goals.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Watch who accepts what colours.
- **Mid:** Protect allies with actions.
- **Late:** Intercept key intel late.

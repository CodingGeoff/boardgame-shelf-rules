## Overview

A realistic experience game: vote on choices in the life of Lee Ji-hye, an ordinary Korean woman; family and friends guard attributes.

## Setup

1. One player is Lee Ji-hye; others take guardian roles (father, mother, brother, friends).

## How to play

1. Read situations, vote on choices, adjust attributes.

## Game end & scoring

- Read the answer booklet and reflect together.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Read the answer booklet and reflect together.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Read situations, vote on choices, adjust attributes.
3. **Setup check:** One player is Lee Ji-hye; others take guardian roles (father, mother, brother, friends).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Serious themes.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Discussion matters more than winning.
- Speak for your attribute.
- Reflect afterwards.

## Common mistakes

- Serious themes.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

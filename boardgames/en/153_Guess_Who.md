## Overview

Two-player yes/no deduction: flip down faces that do not match and guess the opponent's mystery person first.

## Setup

1. Each player draws a mystery person card.

## How to play

1. Ask one yes/no question; flip down non-matching faces.
2. Use a turn to guess; a wrong guess loses.

## Game end & scoring

- First correct guess wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First correct guess wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Ask one yes/no question; flip down non-matching faces.
3. **Setup check:** Each player draws a mystery person card.
4. **The one rule people forget:** A wrong guess loses immediately.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Ask questions that split half.
- Do not guess early.
- Gamble when behind.

## Common mistakes

- A wrong guess loses immediately.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels; people who love deduction and guessing identities; families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

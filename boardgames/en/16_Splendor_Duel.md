## Overview

Two-player Splendor: take gems from a shared board, buy development cards, fight over privileges, and race on three win conditions.

## Setup

1. Fill the gem board in spiral order from the bag.
2. Lay out 3 tiers of cards and royal cards; each player starts with privilege scrolls as listed.

## How to play

1. Optional: use privileges to take single gems; refill the board (opponent gains a privilege).
2. Main action: take up to 3 gems in a line, reserve a card (take gold), or buy a card.
3. Card abilities can give extra turns, steal gems or take privileges.

## Game end & scoring

- Win instantly with 20 points, 10 crowns, or 10 points in a single colour.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win instantly with 20 points, 10 crowns, or 10 points in a single colour.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Optional: use privileges to take single gems; refill the board (opponent gains a privilege).
3. **Setup check:** Fill the gem board in spiral order from the bag.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Hand limit of 10 tokens.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Watch all three win conditions for both players.
- Crowns earn royal cards.
- Avoid leaving a 3-in-a-row of good gems for your opponent.

## Common mistakes

- Hand limit of 10 tokens.
- Taking 3 same-colour gems or 2 pearls gives your opponent a privilege.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

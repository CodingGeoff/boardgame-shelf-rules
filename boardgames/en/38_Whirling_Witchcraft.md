## Overview

Witches play recipe cards simultaneously, transform ingredients on their workbench, and pass cauldron ingredients to the next player.

## Setup

1. Each player has a board (workbench + cauldron) and a witch card; take starting ingredients.
2. Deal 4 recipe cards to each player.

## How to play

1. Everyone simultaneously plays 1 recipe card face down, choosing its direction before reveal.
2. Use each played recipe once per round to transform ingredients.
3. Ingredients placed in your cauldron pass to the next player.

## Game end & scoring

- Complete the goal (see rulebook) — usually first to finish their potion orders.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Complete the goal (see rulebook) — usually first to finish their potion orders.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Everyone simultaneously plays 1 recipe card face down, choosing its direction before reveal.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has a board (workbench + cauldron) and a witch card; take starting ingredients.
4. **The one rule people forget:** You must play a card every round.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Decide recipe direction carefully.
- Build chains that upgrade cheap ingredients.
- Your cauldron feeds your neighbour — do not give what they need.

## Common mistakes

- You must play a card every round.
- Each recipe is used once per round.

## Overview

Each round someone names a resource and everyone must place it in their 4×4 town; build patterns into buildings until you run out of space.

## Setup

1. Each player has a 4×4 board; Cottage plus 6 random building types are used.

## How to play

1. The master builder names a resource; everyone places it.
2. Resources matching a building pattern may be replaced by that building.
3. Monuments are personal buildings.

## Game end & scoring

- When a player cannot place, they are out; score buildings, −1 per empty square.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When a player cannot place, they are out; score buildings, −1 per empty square.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The master builder names a resource; everyone places it.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has a 4×4 board; Cottage plus 6 random building types are used.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Placed resources cannot move.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Name resources hardest for others.
- Overlap patterns to save squares.
- Feed cottages.

## Common mistakes

- Placed resources cannot move.

## Overview

Flip cards while chanting 'taco, cat, goat, cheese, pizza'; when the card matches the word, slap!

## Setup

1. Deal all cards face down.

## How to play

1. Flip your top card while saying the next word in order.
2. If the card matches the word, everyone slaps; last to slap takes the pile.
3. Special cards (gorilla, narwhal, groundhog) require a gesture before slapping.

## Game end & scoring

- Play your last card and win the next slap to win.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Play your last card and win the next slap to win.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Flip your top card while saying the next word in order.
3. **Setup check:** Deal all cards face down.
4. **The one rule people forget:** False slaps take the pile.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Keep a steady rhythm.
- Do the gesture first, then slap.
- Focus when your hand is low.

## Common mistakes

- False slaps take the pile.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

## Overview

Plant trees to make paths, but to score a tree species you must hold the highest sum of it in hand.

## Setup

1. Use 10/8/6 species for 4/3/2 players; deal 7 cards each.

## How to play

1. Draw 2 (from the deck or any discard top).
2. Play 1 to your arboretum orthogonally adjacent.
3. Discard 1 to your own discard pile.

## Game end & scoring

- For each species, the player with the highest hand total of it earns the right to score.
- Paths start and end with the species, strictly increasing; same-colour paths double; a 1 starting a path or an 8 ending it gives bonuses.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** For each species, the player with the highest hand total of it earns the right to score.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw 2 (from the deck or any discard top).
3. **Setup check:** Use 10/8/6 species for 4/3/2 players; deal 7 cards each.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Ties for highest sum: all tied players score.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Discards feed opponents.
- Paths must begin and end with the scoring species.
- Your 1 zeroes an opponent's 8 in hand totals.

## Common mistakes

- Ties for highest sum: all tied players score.

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Draw 2, play 1 to my arboretum, discard 1.
2. Cards are placed adjacent, forming paths.
3. At the end, scoring rights per species go to the highest hand total.
4. A 1 beats an 8 if the opponent holds the 8.

### Scoring example

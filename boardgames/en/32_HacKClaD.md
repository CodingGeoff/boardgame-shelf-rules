## Overview

Witches battle the giant Clad together but compete for points: deck-building plus arena positioning.

## Setup

1. Each witch has a starter deck and board; set up Clad, the arena and mission cards (2 per player).

## How to play

1. Forecast: reveal 3 Clad action cards.
2. Draft turn order: insert your turn between Clad's three actions.
3. Witch turn: play cards to move, attack and cast; spend CP on strong skills.
4. Clad acts in card order; hit witches take wounds.

## Game end & scoring

- After the final round, score damage, missions and wound penalties; highest wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After the final round, score damage, missions and wound penalties; highest wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Forecast: reveal 3 Clad action cards.
3. **Setup check:** Each witch has a starter deck and board; set up Clad, the arena and mission cards (2 per player).
4. **The one rule people forget:** CP max 7; wounds unlimited and cost points.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Read the forecast and pre-position safely.
- Being hit on your own turn ends it — move first, then attack.
- Deck reform gives +1 CP.

## Common mistakes

- CP max 7; wounds unlimited and cost points.
- CROSS×FATE requires the base game.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** experienced gamers who want a deep think
- **Not ideal for:** relaxed chatting sessions; when time is short

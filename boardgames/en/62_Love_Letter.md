## Overview

A 16-card game: hold one card, draw one, play one; guard guesses, baron compares, prince discards, deliver the letter to the princess.

## Setup

1. Shuffle; remove one card face down; each player draws 1.

## How to play

1. Draw 1 and play 1, resolving its effect.
2. Guard: guess a hand (not Guard); Priest: look; Baron: compare; Handmaid: protection; Prince: discard and redraw; King: swap; Countess: must be played with King/Prince; Princess: discarded = out.

## Game end & scoring

- Last player standing or highest card when the deck runs out wins a token; play to the token target.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Last player standing or highest card when the deck runs out wins a token; play to the token target.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw 1 and play 1, resolving its effect.
3. **Setup check:** Shuffle; remove one card face down; each player draws 1.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Guard cannot guess Guard.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Count played cards for Guard guesses.
- Countess rules allow bluffing.
- Avoid holding the Princess against Princes.

## Common mistakes

- Guard cannot guess Guard.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** people who love deduction and guessing identities; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 20–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 30 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Hold one card. Draw one, play one and resolve: Guard guesses a hand; Priest peeks; Baron compares; Handmaid protects; Prince forces a discard; King swaps; Countess must be played with King/Prince; discarding the Princess eliminates you. Last standing or highest card wins a token.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Use Guards and Priests early for info.
- **Mid:** Track played cards to deduce what's left.
- **Late:** Holding the Princess? Beware the Prince.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Can a Guard name Guard?**  
  A: No.
- **Q: Tokens to win?**  
  A: Depends on player count.

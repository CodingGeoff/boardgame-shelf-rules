## Overview

Sid Sackson's pure negotiation classic: deals need specific investors; the boss haggles the split, and influence cards send investors travelling or seize the deal.

## Setup

1. Deal the 6 investor cards face up (extras aside in small games); deal 5 influence cards each.
2. Stack deal cards by number; the first player is earliest alphabetically by investor; the player on their right places the dollar marker on any space.

## How to play

1. On your turn: negotiate the deal where the dollar marker is, OR roll to move the marker clockwise (counting only unclosed deals) then negotiate or draw 3 influence cards.
2. The initiator is the boss and must assemble all required investors.
3. Profit = dividends on the space × current share price; the boss and participants negotiate the split — no advance payments.
4. During negotiation anyone may play influence cards (send investors travelling, take over as boss, etc.).
5. When all investors are in, the boss announces terms and says 'going once, twice — deal!'; no more influence cards, the bank pays.
6. If required investors are all travelling and nobody can substitute, the deal fails.

## Game end & scoring

- After the final deal card, the richest player wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After the final deal card, the richest player wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** On your turn: negotiate the deal where the dollar marker is, OR roll to move the marker clockwise (counting only unclosed deals) then negotiate or draw 3 influence cards.
3. **Setup check:** Deal the 6 investor cards face up (extras aside in small games); deal 5 influence cards each.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Promises are non-binding and nothing is paid in advance.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Scarce investors give leverage.
- Play 'I'm the Boss' after others agree.
- Credibility is long-term capital.

## Common mistakes

- Promises are non-binding and nothing is paid in advance.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 60–60 min; budget about 70 min for a first game.
- Total: about 80 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Pure negotiation. Each deal needs certain investor families; haggle over shares and disrupt with cards — send families on holiday, swap them. Most money wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Collect families and cards.
- **Mid:** Control key families.
- **Late:** Max shares in the last deals.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Are deals binding?**  
  A: Agreed shares must be paid.
- **Q: Can the boss pass?**  
  A: Yes, no profit.

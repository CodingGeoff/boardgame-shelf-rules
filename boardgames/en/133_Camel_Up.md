## Overview

Bet on a camel race where camels stack and carry those on top; bet on legs and overall winner and loser.

## Setup

1. Place camels; leg betting tiles; pyramid of dice.

## How to play

1. Choose one: take a leg bet, place a spectator tile, roll a pyramid die, bet on overall winner/loser.

## Game end & scoring

- When a camel crosses the finish, settle overall bets; most money wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When a camel crosses the finish, settle overall bets; most money wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Choose one: take a leg bet, place a spectator tile, roll a pyramid die, bet on overall winner/loser.
3. **Setup check:** Place camels; leg betting tiles; pyramid of dice.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Crazy camels move backwards (2nd ed.).
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Top of the stack has the edge.
- Bet early on legs.
- Rolling earns 1 coin.

## Common mistakes

- Crazy camels move backwards (2nd ed.).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Overview

Two hidden characters equal two influences: claim characters to take actions; challenges reveal the truth.

## Setup

1. Each player takes 2 face-down characters and 2 coins.

## How to play

1. General actions: Income (+1), Foreign Aid (+2, blockable by Duke), Coup (7 coins, mandatory at 10).
2. Character actions: Duke tax +3; Assassin pays 3 to kill; Captain steals 2; Ambassador exchanges; Contessa blocks assassination.
3. Any action or block may be challenged; the loser of a challenge loses an influence.

## Game end & scoring

- Lose both influences and you are out; last player wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Lose both influences and you are out; last player wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** General actions: Income (+1), Foreign Aid (+2, blockable by Duke), Coup (7 coins, mandatory at 10).
3. **Setup check:** Each player takes 2 face-down characters and 2 coins.
4. **The one rule people forget:** 10 coins forces a coup.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Fake Duke is common — and commonly challenged.
- Track revealed characters.
- Near 7 coins you become a target.

## Common mistakes

- 10 coins forces a coup.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

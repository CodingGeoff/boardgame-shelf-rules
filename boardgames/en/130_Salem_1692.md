## Overview

Witch trials: accusations force trial cards to be revealed; witches convert at night; townsfolk must find all witches.

## Setup

1. Deal tryal cards (witch, constable, not a witch) and public character cards.

## How to play

1. Draw 2 or play cards (accusations, evidence, blue/green/red cards).
2. Seven accusation points force a tryal reveal.
3. Night: witches kill; constable protects.

## Game end & scoring

- Players with all tryals revealed die; teams win per rulebook.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Players with all tryals revealed die; teams win per rulebook.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw 2 or play cards (accusations, evidence, blue/green/red cards).
3. **Setup check:** Deal tryal cards (witch, constable, not a witch) and public character cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** All tryal cards revealed = death.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Accuse suspects to force reveals.
- The constable protects at night.
- Find the original witch early.

## Common mistakes

- All tryal cards revealed = death.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

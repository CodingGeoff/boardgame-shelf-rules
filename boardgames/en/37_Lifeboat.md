## Overview

Survival on a lifeboat: secret love and hate, rowing, thirst, grabbing supplies and throwing people overboard.

## Setup

1. Seat characters (front seats are more powerful); deal secret Friend and Enemy cards.
2. Shuffle supplies and navigation cards.

## How to play

1. Each round: grab supplies, row or swap seats, fight (strength + weapons), and navigate (those overboard get thirsty).
2. Fights may involve helpers on either side.
3. Rowers become tired.

## Game end & scoring

- When land is reached, score survivors, your beloved and items; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When land is reached, score survivors, your beloved and items; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Each round: grab supplies, row or swap seats, fight (strength + weapons), and navigate (those overboard get thirsty).
3. **Setup check:** Seat characters (front seats are more powerful); deal secret Friend and Enemy cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Rowing causes fatigue.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Keeping your love alive matters more than your own survival.
- Gather allies before fights.
- Control navigation picks to decide who gets thirsty.

## Common mistakes

- Rowing causes fatigue.
- Compare total strength including helpers.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 60–90 min; budget about 110 min for a first game.
- Total: about 125 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> A lifeboat crammed with survivors — each has a secret love and a secret enemy. Each day row, drink and share supplies; people get thirsty, fight, or are thrown overboard. When land is reached, score for survivors you love, yourself, items, and dead enemies.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Watch who favours whom to read loves and enemies.
- **Mid:** Grab useful items and form alliances.
- **Late:** Near land, decide whether to betray.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Can I score if dead?**  
  A: Yes, from loves/enemies; see rulebook.

## Overview

Build an 18-level tower; take turns removing a block with one hand and placing it on top; whoever topples it loses.

## Setup

1. Build 18 levels of 3 blocks, alternating directions.

## How to play

1. Use one hand at a time.
2. Remove a block below the top completed level and place it on top.
3. Your turn ends after 10 seconds or when the next player touches the tower.

## Game end & scoring

- The player who makes the tower fall loses.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** The player who makes the tower fall loses.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Use one hand at a time.
3. **Setup check:** Build 18 levels of 3 blocks, alternating directions.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Do not take from the top incomplete level or the one below it.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Probe for loose blocks.
- Centre blocks keep balance.
- Finish the top level properly.

## Common mistakes

- Do not take from the top incomplete level or the one below it.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; families with young children; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

## Overview

The harbour master auction: place workers on boats, ports, shipyards, pirates or navigators; roll dice to move boats.

## Setup

1. Auction harbour master; distribute shares.

## How to play

1. Harbour master chooses cargo and starting positions.
2. Place workers in turn.
3. Roll dice three times to move boats; resolve payouts.

## Game end & scoring

- When a cargo price reaches 30, the game ends; richest wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When a cargo price reaches 30, the game ends; richest wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Harbour master chooses cargo and starting positions.
3. **Setup check:** Auction harbour master; distribute shares.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Harbour master has privileges.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Load high-value cargo.
- Navigators decide landings.
- Pirates raid at space 13.

## Common mistakes

## Overview

The harbour master auction: place workers on boats, ports, shipyards, pirates or navigators; roll dice to move boats.

## Setup

1. Auction harbour master; distribute shares.

## How to play

1. Harbour master chooses cargo and starting positions.
2. Place workers in turn.
3. Roll dice three times to move boats; resolve payouts.

## Game end & scoring

- When a cargo price reaches 30, the game ends; richest wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When a cargo price reaches 30, the game ends; richest wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Harbour master chooses cargo and starting positions.
3. **Setup check:** Auction harbour master; distribute shares.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Harbour master has privileges.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Load high-value cargo.
- Navigators decide landings.
- Pirates raid at space 13.

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 60–90 min; budget about 110 min for a first game.
- Total: about 120 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Auction to become harbourmaster, buy shares and set cargo. Everyone places workers on boats, ports, shipyards, pilots or pirates. Roll three times to move boats. Arriving boats pay out and raise share values.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Bid for harbourmaster wisely.
- **Mid:** Spread workers to reduce risk.
- **Late:** Calculate before selling.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Pirates?**  
  A: Board ships after roll 2.
- **Q: Mortgage shares?**  
  A: Yes.

## Overview

Housemates cooperate to decorate: each has secret conditions and can only react 'happy' or 'unhappy' to changes.

## Setup

1. Set up the house; choose a scenario; deal secret conditions.

## How to play

1. On your turn make one change (add, swap, remove).
2. Others react only with approval or disapproval.

## Game end & scoring

- Win if all conditions are satisfied within the limit.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win if all conditions are satisfied within the limit.
2. **What you do on a turn:** On your turn make one change (add, swap, remove).
3. **Setup check:** Set up the house; choose a scenario; deal secret conditions.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Never state conditions.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Change one thing at a time.
- Log reactions.
- Compromise conflicting goals.

## Common mistakes

- Never state conditions.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 30–45 min; budget about 55 min for a first game.
- Total: about 70 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Housemates redecorate cooperatively. Each has secret conditions. On your turn change something (add, remove, swap); others can only react with approval or displeasure. Win when all conditions are met.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Meet your easiest conditions first.
- **Mid:** Read reactions to infer others' needs.
- **Late:** Fine-tune at the end.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Can I state my conditions?**  
  A: No.
- **Q: How many scenarios?**  
  A: Many, increasing difficulty.

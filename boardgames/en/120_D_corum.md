## Overview

Housemates cooperate to decorate: each has secret conditions and can only react 'happy' or 'unhappy' to changes.

## Setup

1. Set up the house; choose a scenario; deal secret conditions.

## How to play

1. On your turn make one change (add, swap, remove).
2. Others react only with approval or disapproval.

## Game end & scoring

- Win if all conditions are satisfied within the limit.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win if all conditions are satisfied within the limit.
2. **What you do on a turn:** On your turn make one change (add, swap, remove).
3. **Setup check:** Set up the house; choose a scenario; deal secret conditions.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Never state conditions.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Change one thing at a time.
- Log reactions.
- Compromise conflicting goals.

## Common mistakes

- Never state conditions.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Overview

Find three of a kind: ask anyone to reveal their lowest or highest card, or flip a centre card.

## Setup

1. Deal by player count; hands sorted ascending; remainder face down in the centre.

## How to play

1. Reveal cards one at a time: someone's lowest or highest, or a centre card.
2. Stop on a mismatch and turn the cards back.
3. Three matching: collect the trio.

## Game end & scoring

- Win with 3 trios, or 2 that add/subtract to 7, or the trio of 7s (rule variants).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win with 3 trios, or 2 that add/subtract to 7, or the trio of 7s (rule variants).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Reveal cards one at a time: someone's lowest or highest, or a centre card.
3. **Setup check:** Deal by player count; hands sorted ascending; remainder face down in the centre.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Two different numbers end your turn.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Memory is everything.
- Your own hand is also a valid target.
- Watch for 7s.

## Common mistakes

- Two different numbers end your turn.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** people who love deduction and guessing identities; families with young children; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 15–15 min; budget about 20 min for a first game.
- Total: about 25 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Cards are split between hands and a face-down centre. On your turn ask anyone to reveal their lowest or highest card, or flip a centre card. Three matching numbers in a row wins a trio; a mismatch ends your turn and cards go back. Win with 3 trios, the 7 trio, or two trios summing/differing to 7 (check edition).

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Start with safe reveals of your own extremes.
- **Mid:** Memorise every revealed card's location.
- **Late:** Track where the 7s are.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: What happens after a mismatch?**  
  A: All cards return face down.
- **Q: Can I ask again?**  
  A: Yes, though extremes may have changed.

## Overview

Four face-down cards of which you peek at two; swap and use powers, then call CABO when you think you are lowest.

## Setup

1. Each player lays out 4 face-down cards and peeks at 2.

## How to play

1. Draw from the deck or take the discard.
2. Discard it, replace one of your cards, or use its power (7–8 peek yours, 9–10 peek another, 11–12 swap).
3. Call CABO instead of drawing; others take one last turn.

## Game end & scoring

- Lowest total scores 0; the CABO caller is penalised if not lowest; play to 100 points.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Lowest total scores 0; the CABO caller is penalised if not lowest; play to 100 points.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw from the deck or take the discard.
3. **Setup check:** Each player lays out 4 face-down cards and peeks at 2.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The caller cannot act after calling.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- 13s are deadly — swap them.
- Call only when confident.
- Multiple identical cards may be replaced together (per rules).

## Common mistakes

- The caller cannot act after calling.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

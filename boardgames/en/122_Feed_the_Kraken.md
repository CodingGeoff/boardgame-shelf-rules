## Overview

Three factions on a pirate ship — sailors, pirates and cultists — want different destinations; elect officers, navigate and vote.

## Setup

1. Deal secret loyalties by player count.

## How to play

1. The captain nominates a lieutenant and navigator; others vote.
2. Navigation: officers secretly contribute direction cards.
3. Actions: mutiny, flogging, feeding someone to the kraken; the cult leader converts.

## Game end & scoring

- Ship reaches blue water (sailors), red (pirates), or the kraken (cultists).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Ship reaches blue water (sailors), red (pirates), or the kraken (cultists).
2. **What you do on a turn:** The captain nominates a lieutenant and navigator; others vote.
3. **Setup check:** Deal secret loyalties by player count.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Different setups by player count.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Nominations are trust votes.
- Track navigation cards.
- Conversions can flip the game.

## Common mistakes

- Different setups by player count.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

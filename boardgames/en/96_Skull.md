## Overview

Three roses and one skull each: place discs face down, then bid how many you can flip without hitting a skull.

## Setup

1. Each player has a mat and 4 discs (3 roses, 1 skull).

## How to play

1. Place a disc face down; continue placing or start a bid.
2. Bids rise; the highest bidder flips their own discs first, then others'.
3. Success: flip your mat (2 = win). Skull: lose a random disc.

## Game end & scoring

- Two successful challenges or last player with discs wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Two successful challenges or last player with discs wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Place a disc face down; continue placing or start a bid.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has a mat and 4 discs (3 roses, 1 skull).
4. **The one rule people forget:** The skull's owner chooses which of your discs you lose.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Place your skull then bid high to scare.
- You flip your own first — careful.
- Watch escalating bidders.

## Common mistakes

- The skull's owner chooses which of your discs you lose.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. 4 coasters: 3 roses, 1 skull.
2. Everyone places a coaster face down.
3. I bid: 'I can flip 3 without a skull.'
4. Others raise or pass; I must flip mine first.
5. I hit a skull and lose a coaster.

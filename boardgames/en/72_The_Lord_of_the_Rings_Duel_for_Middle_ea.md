## Overview

By the 7 Wonders Duel team: pyramid card drafting with three paths to victory: carry the Ring, rally the races, or conquer Middle-earth.

## Setup

1. Choose Fellowship or Sauron; place starting armies; set up the quest track.

## How to play

1. Take an accessible card from the chapter layout and play it (or discard for coins).
2. Resources and chain symbols reduce costs.
3. Cards move the quest track, add race support, or place armies on the map.

## Game end & scoring

- Instant wins: Ring to Mount Doom / Fellowship caught; 6 different race symbols; or presence in all regions (see rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Instant wins: Ring to Mount Doom / Fellowship caught; 6 different race symbols; or presence in all regions (see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Take an accessible card from the chapter layout and play it (or discard for coins).
3. **Setup check:** Choose Fellowship or Sauron; place starting armies; set up the quest track.
4. **The one rule people forget:** All three victories end the game immediately.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Deny the opponent's closest win path.
- Chains make later cards free.
- Armies decide region control.

## Common mistakes

- All three victories end the game immediately.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 30–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 50 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> From the 7 Wonders Duel team. Draft from a pyramid; three win paths: carry the Ring to Mount Doom, gather 6 races, or conquer Middle-earth. Switch paths to exploit weaknesses.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Develop evenly and read the opponent.
- **Mid:** Use the pyramid to leave bad picks.
- **Late:** Sprint the closest path.

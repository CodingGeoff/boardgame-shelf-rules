## Overview

Dwarves dig tunnels for gold, but saboteurs secretly block paths and break tools.

## Setup

1. Deal role cards by player count; place the start and 3 goal cards (1 gold).

## How to play

1. Play a path card, an action card (break/repair tools, rockfall, map), or pass by discarding.
2. Paths must connect from the start.

## Game end & scoring

- Reaching gold rewards the miners; otherwise saboteurs win; play 3 rounds and count gold.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Reaching gold rewards the miners; otherwise saboteurs win; play 3 rounds and count gold.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play a path card, an action card (break/repair tools, rockfall, map), or pass by discarding.
3. **Setup check:** Deal role cards by player count; place the start and 3 goal cards (1 gold).
4. **The one rule people forget:** You cannot play paths with a broken tool.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Lie or tell the truth about maps with purpose.
- Saboteurs keep a blocking card for the key moment.
- Broken tools do not prove guilt.

## Common mistakes

- You cannot play paths with a broken tool.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

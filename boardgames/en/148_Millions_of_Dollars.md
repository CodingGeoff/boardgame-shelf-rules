## Overview

Gang loot split: everyone secretly picks a role; duplicated roles are all eliminated; unique roles use abilities. First to 20 million wins.

## Setup

1. Each player takes a family (8 families, same roles, different art): role cards plus matching henchman cards, 5×1M and an aid card.
2. Draw 8 loot cards face down; threat cards aside; give the leader card to one player.

## How to play

1. Plan: reveal a loot card (8–12M total, a 1M or 2M deposit, a favoured role); secretly choose a role face down with your deposit and pass the matching henchman card to the centre.
2. Negotiate: talk, threaten, bribe; quitters take back their deposit. Discard a threat card to peek at a player's role.
3. Rob: resolve Informant → Thug → others. Duplicates are all eliminated; unique roles act.
4. Roles: Thug (always; alone takes a threat card), Driver (always; collects 1M fare from each other sharer), Villain (always; robs the Thug of 2M), Informant (5+; alone recovers deposit and eliminates a role; fined 3M if left alone), Mastermind (7+; alone adds 2M).

## Game end & scoring

- First to 20M wins (some versions 15M at 3–4p), or richest when the 8 loot cards are used.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to 20M wins (some versions 15M at 3–4p), or richest when the 8 loot cards are used.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Plan: reveal a loot card (8–12M total, a 1M or 2M deposit, a favoured role); secretly choose a role face down with your deposit and pass the matching henchman card to the centre.
3. **Setup check:** Each player takes a family (8 families, same roles, different art): role cards plus matching henchman cards, 5×1M and an aid card.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Any duplicates are eliminated.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Steer others with promises.
- Popular roles collide; quiet ones survive.
- Watch for bluffs.

## Common mistakes

- Any duplicates are eliminated.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

## Overview

Bag-building: draw ingredients into your pot; more than 7 in white cherry bombs explodes it; buy better ingredients.

## Setup

1. Each player has a pot, a bag of starting ingredients and a flask.

## How to play

1. All draw simultaneously, placing ingredients along the spiral.
2. White total over 7 explodes: choose points or buying.
3. Effects by colour book; rubies and fortune events.

## Game end & scoring

- After 9 rounds, most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 9 rounds, most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** All draw simultaneously, placing ingredients along the spiral.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has a pot, a bag of starting ingredients and a flask.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Exactly 7 white does not explode.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Buy efficient low-cost ingredients early.
- Compute explosion risk.
- Rat tails help trailing players.

## Common mistakes

- Exactly 7 white does not explode.

- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 45–45 min; budget about 55 min for a first game.
- Total: about 65 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Bag-building potion making. Draw ingredients one at a time; white cherry bombs over 7 explode the pot. Stop anytime to score and buy. If you explode you choose points or shopping, not both. Most VP after 9 rounds wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Buy cheap ingredients early.
- **Mid:** Add strong colours mid-game.
- **Late:** Push harder late.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Explode loses everything?**  
  A: No — choose one.
- **Q: What are rubies for?**  
  A: Move droplet or refill flask.

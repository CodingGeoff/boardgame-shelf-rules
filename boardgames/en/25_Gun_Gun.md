## Overview

A two-player duel with two personified guns each: load ammo, fire each gun once per turn, and empty your opponent's 30 HP.

## Setup

1. Choose a Gunner and 2 Guns; each gun has a 20-card deck; combine them as your deck.
2. Start at 30 HP.

## How to play

1. Play ammo, skill and equipment cards.
2. Load ammo into a gun.
3. Fire: each gun can fire once per turn, dealing HIT damage and triggering effects.
4. Draw or refill at the end of turn.

## Game end & scoring

- Reduce your opponent to 0 HP.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Reduce your opponent to 0 HP.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play ammo, skill and equipment cards.
3. **Setup check:** Choose a Gunner and 2 Guns; each gun has a 20-card deck; combine them as your deck.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Each gun fires at most once per turn.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Your two guns define your style — burst, sustain or control.
- Decide whether to fire both guns or keep ammo for reactions.
- OVERHEAT's Burn deals 3 HIT per marker at the opponent's turn start.

## Common mistakes

- Each gun fires at most once per turn.
- Burn removal rules per rulebook.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 20–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 45 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Each player dual-wields two personified guns with unique abilities. Reload, then fire each gun once per turn. Some excel at range, some at burst. First to drain the opponent's 30 HP wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Learn your guns' synergy — steady plus burst.
- **Mid:** Manage ammo; don't empty out and get punished.
- **Late:** Burst when the opponent is low.

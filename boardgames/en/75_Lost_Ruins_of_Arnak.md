## Overview

Deck-building plus worker placement: send archaeologists to dig sites, discover new places, beat guardians and advance research over 5 rounds.

## Setup

1. Choose the bird or snake temple side; set the market, sites and idols.
2. Each player: board, 2 archaeologists, research tokens, 4 starter cards and resources by seat.

## How to play

1. One main action per turn: dig, discover a new site, overcome a guardian, buy a card, play a card, research; then pass when done.
2. Resources: coins, compasses, tablets, arrowheads, jewels.

## Game end & scoring

- After 5 rounds, score research, temple, idols, guardians, cards, minus fear; most wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 5 rounds, score research, temple, idols, guardians, cards, minus fear; most wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** One main action per turn: dig, discover a new site, overcome a guardian, buy a card, play a card, research; then pass when done.
3. **Setup check:** Choose the bird or snake temple side; set the market, sites and idols.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Two archaeologists (a third via research).
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Research is the main point source.
- Items improve your deck; artifacts give immediate effects.
- Beat guardians when you can or face fear.

## Common mistakes

- Two archaeologists (a third via research).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** most board gamers
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

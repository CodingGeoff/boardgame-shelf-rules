## Overview

The conductor chooses which track to run over; teams add innocents to their track, guilty people to the other, and modifiers — then argue.

## Setup

1. Choose a conductor; split others into two teams; shuffle the three decks.

## How to play

1. Each team draws 3 innocents, 3 guilty, 3 modifiers.
2. Reveal one innocent per track; each team adds an innocent to its track and a guilty to the opponents'.
3. Players place modifiers on any character.
4. Teams argue; the conductor pulls the lever; the hit team gets death tokens.

## Game end & scoring

- Rotate the conductor; fewest deaths (or per variant) wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Rotate the conductor; fewest deaths (or per variant) wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Each team draws 3 innocents, 3 guilty, 3 modifiers.
3. **Setup check:** Choose a conductor; split others into two teams; shuffle the three decks.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Adult content; choose your audience.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Use modifiers to flip the other team's innocents.
- Argue to the conductor's values.
- Humour persuades.

## Common mistakes

- Adult content; choose your audience.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

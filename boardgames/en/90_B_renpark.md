## Overview

Build your bear park with polyomino tiles: covering icons grants new tiles and park sections; early bear enclosures score more.

## Setup

1. Each player starts with a 4×4 park section; central supply of tiles.

## How to play

1. Place a tile; icons covered give rewards (green areas, animal houses, enclosures, new sections).
2. Completed sections earn bear statues.

## Game end & scoring

- When a player completes their park, finish the round; score tiles and statues.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When a player completes their park, finish the round; score tiles and statues.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Place a tile; icons covered give rewards (green areas, animal houses, enclosures, new sections).
3. **Setup check:** Each player starts with a 4×4 park section; central supply of tiles.
4. **The one rule people forget:** New sections must be adjacent.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Grab high-value enclosures early.
- Plan section entrances.
- Statues score by completion order.

## Common mistakes

- New sections must be adjacent.

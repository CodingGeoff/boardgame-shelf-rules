## Overview

The Industrial Revolution over the canal and rail eras: build industries, links and sell goods using loans.

## Setup

1. Set up the market, merchants and cards; each player has a board and money.

## How to play

1. Two actions per turn (one in the first): build, network, develop, sell, loan, scout.
2. Coal and iron consumption follow connection rules.
3. Turn order: least money spent goes first.

## Game end & scoring

- After each era score links and flipped industries; the canal era removes canals and level-1 industries; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After each era score links and flipped industries; the canal era removes canals and level-1 industries; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Two actions per turn (one in the first): build, network, develop, sell, loan, scout.
3. **Setup check:** Set up the market, merchants and cards; each player has a board and money.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Loans reduce income level.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Cotton and pottery need merchants to flip.
- Use others' coal and iron.
- Claim key routes in the canal era.

## Common mistakes

- Loans reduce income level.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** experienced gamers who want a deep think
- **Not ideal for:** relaxed chatting sessions

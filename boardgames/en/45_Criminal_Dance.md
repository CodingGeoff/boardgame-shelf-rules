## Overview

Hand-passing deduction: the detective must name the culprit before the culprit escapes by playing his card last.

## Setup

1. Deal cards by player count, including First Discoverer and Culprit.

## How to play

1. The First Discoverer starts.
2. Play a card and resolve: Trade, Rumour (pass cards), Detective (accuse), Alibi, Witness, Accomplice, etc.
3. The Culprit card can only be played as your last card.

## Game end & scoring

- Detective accuses correctly (and no Alibi): detective's side wins.
- Culprit played last: culprit and accomplices win.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Detective accuses correctly (and no Alibi): detective's side wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The First Discoverer starts.
3. **Setup check:** Deal cards by player count, including First Discoverer and Culprit.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Alibi only works when you hold it at the accusation.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Track where the Culprit card moves.
- Check alibis before accusing.
- Accomplices protect the culprit.

## Common mistakes

- Alibi only works when you hold it at the accusation.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 10–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 30 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Deal 4 cards each. The First Discoverer leads; players play one card at a time. The Culprit can only be played as your last card. A Detective card accuses someone — correct guesses win. Trade and Rumour cards shuffle cards around.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Watch who holds onto a card — maybe the Culprit.
- **Mid:** Use trades and rumours to move or track the Culprit.
- **Late:** Hold the Detective until confident.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: What about Alibi?**  
  A: An Alibi holder can deny the accusation.
- **Q: How does the Accomplice win?**  
  A: With the Culprit.

## Overview

The forensic scientist knows the truth but can only use clue tiles; investigators deduce the means and key evidence; the murderer misleads.

## Setup

1. Deal roles (scientist, murderer, investigators; optional accomplice and witness); each investigator shows means and clue cards.

## How to play

1. The murderer secretly selects a means and evidence.
2. The scientist places bullets on scene tiles.
3. Each player has one badge to solve.

## Game end & scoring

- Correct solution: investigators win; otherwise murderer wins (witness rules optional).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Correct solution: investigators win; otherwise murderer wins (witness rules optional).
2. **What you do on a turn:** The murderer secretly selects a means and evidence.
3. **Setup check:** Deal roles (scientist, murderer, investigators; optional accomplice and witness); each investigator shows means and clue cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The scientist may not speak.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Scientist narrows with cause and location tiles first.
- Murderer steers attention.
- One guess only.

## Common mistakes

- The scientist may not speak.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 20–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 35 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> The forensic scientist knows the truth but only marks clue tiles. Investigators deduce the means and key evidence; the murderer misleads. Correct guess wins for investigators.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Forensic: pick clear clues.
- **Mid:** Investigators: cross-reference.
- **Late:** Guess carefully.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Guesses per player?**  
  A: One.

  A: Yes, to mislead.

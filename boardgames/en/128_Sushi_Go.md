## Overview

Draft sushi: pick one, pass the rest; score tempura, sashimi, dumplings, nigiri with wasabi; pudding at the end.

## Setup

1. Deal hands by player count.

## How to play

1. Pick a card simultaneously and pass the rest.
2. Chopsticks let you take two later.

## Game end & scoring

- Score each round; after round 3, puddings: most +6, fewest −6.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Score each round; after round 3, puddings: most +6, fewest −6.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Pick a card simultaneously and pass the rest.
3. **Setup check:** Deal hands by player count.
4. **The one rule people forget:** No pudding penalty in 2-player.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Count remaining sets.
- Wasabi + squid = 9.
- Do not ignore puddings.

## Common mistakes

- No pudding penalty in 2-player.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Overview

A two-player bakumatsu spy duel: six face-down agents with hidden values and real or fake intel move with location cards and capture by collision.

## Setup

1. Each side has 6 agents: 3 (real), 3 (fake), 2 (real), 2 (fake), 1 (fake), 0 (real); real intel shows a red dot.
2. Place 5 agents on your back row and 1 in front of the middle space, values facing you.
3. Shuffle the location and tactic decks; each player draws 4 location + 2 tactic cards (6 total).

## How to play

1. Play 1 card, then refill to 6 from either deck.
2. Location cards: move one agent 1 space forward or diagonally forward onto a space of the card's colour (wild cards excepted). No sideways or backward moves.
3. Your agents cannot share a space and cannot enter the enemy HQ unless an ability says so.
4. Conflict: moving onto an enemy agent makes you the attacker. The defender reveals; the attacker states its value truthfully without revealing.
5. Compare: 3 > 2 > 1 > 0, but 0 beats 3. The attacker wins on greater or equal and captures the defender.
6. Tactic cards (e.g. Defence) allow special moves such as stepping sideways or back.

## Game end & scoring

- Win by capturing enough real intel, or by getting a real-intel agent into the enemy HQ (see rulebook for exact conditions).
- Advanced play adds charm and equipment cards.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win by capturing enough real intel, or by getting a real-intel agent into the enemy HQ (see rulebook for exact conditions).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play 1 card, then refill to 6 from either deck.
3. **Setup check:** Each side has 6 agents: 3 (real), 3 (fake), 2 (real), 2 (fake), 1 (fake), 0 (real); real intel shows a red dot.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Location cards only allow moves onto matching colours.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- 0 beats 3 — hide your 0 where the enemy 3 will travel.
- Attackers only announce values, so attacking keeps your real/fake status secret.
- Save Defence-type tactics for when an agent is cornered.

## Common mistakes

- Location cards only allow moves onto matching colours.
- Check the rulebook for what happens when both decks run out.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels; talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

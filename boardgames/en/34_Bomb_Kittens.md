## Overview

Draw a bomb cat and you are out unless you defuse it; use skip, peek and attack cards to push the danger onto others.

## Setup

1. Deal hands with one defuse each; shuffle bombs (players − 1) into the deck.

## How to play

1. Play any number of action cards, then draw 1 card to end your turn.
2. Drawing a bomb: play a defuse and reinsert it anywhere secretly, or you are out.
3. Nope cards cancel actions and can chain.

## Game end & scoring

- Last player remaining wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Last player remaining wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play any number of action cards, then draw 1 card to end your turn.
3. **Setup check:** Deal hands with one defuse each; shuffle bombs (players − 1) into the deck.
4. **The one rule people forget:** You must draw at the end unless skipped or attacking.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- See the Future + Shuffle/Skip is safest.
- Reinsert defused bombs to target the next player.
- Keep a Nope for attacks.

## Common mistakes

- You must draw at the end unless skipped or attacking.
- Attack gives the next player two turns.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 15–15 min; budget about 20 min for a first game.
- Total: about 25 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Draw an Exploding Kitten and you're out unless you have a Defuse. On your turn play any number of action cards, then draw to end your turn. Skip (no draw), Attack (next player takes 2 turns), See the Future, Shuffle, Favour... Last one alive wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Protect your Defuse.
- **Mid:** See the Future, then Skip/Attack to dodge.
- **Late:** Late game, hoard Skips and Attacks.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Where does a defused kitten go?**  
  A: Anywhere in the deck, secretly.
- **Q: Can Nope cancel Nope?**  
  A: Yes.

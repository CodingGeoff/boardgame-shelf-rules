## Overview

Roll around the board, buy cities, build and collect rent; chance cards add twists.

## Setup

1. Choose a banker; give equal starting money (often 1500).

## How to play

1. Roll and move; collect salary passing GO.
2. Buy unowned property or auction it; pay rent on owned property.
3. Build houses with full colour sets; chance, fate and jail rules.

## Game end & scoring

- Last solvent player wins, or richest at a time limit.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Last solvent player wins, or richest at a time limit.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Roll and move; collect salary passing GO.
3. **Setup check:** Choose a banker; give equal starting money (often 1500).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Amounts vary by edition.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Complete colour sets.
- Cash flow matters.
- Agree a time limit.

## Common mistakes

- Amounts vary by edition.
- Unbought properties go to auction (official rule).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

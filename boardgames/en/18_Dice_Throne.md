## Overview

A fighting-game-style dice duel: each hero has unique dice faces and an ability board.

## Setup

1. Each player chooses a hero, takes their board, dice, cards and tokens; set health (usually 50) and CP.
2. Draw 4 cards.

## How to play

1. Upkeep and income: gain 1 CP and draw 1 card.
2. Main phase: play cards or sell them for CP.
3. Offensive roll: roll 5 dice up to 3 times (Yahtzee style) to activate an ability.
4. Targeted opponent may make a defensive roll; resolve damage and status effects.
5. Second main phase and discard down to hand limit.

## Game end & scoring

- Reduce your opponent's health to 0.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Reduce your opponent's health to 0.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Upkeep and income: gain 1 CP and draw 1 card.
3. **Setup check:** Each player chooses a hero, takes their board, dice, cards and tokens; set health (usually 50) and CP.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Undefendable damage cannot be reduced by defence.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Learn your hero's ultimate combination.
- Cards that modify dice are key; time them.
- Status effects can outweigh raw damage.

## Common mistakes

- Undefendable damage cannot be reduced by defence.
- Respect the hand limit (6).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

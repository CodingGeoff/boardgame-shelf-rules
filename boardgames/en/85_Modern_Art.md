## Overview

Knizia's auction classic: auction paintings in five formats and drive artists' values; only top artists each season pay.

## Setup

1. Each player has $100 behind a screen; deal painting cards (5 artists).

## How to play

1. Take turns as auctioneer, playing a painting with its auction type: open, once around, sealed, fixed price, or double.
2. Money goes to the auctioneer (or to the bank if the auctioneer buys).
3. A season ends when an artist's 5th painting is played.

## Game end & scoring

- Top three artists score 30/20/10 cumulatively; sell paintings to the bank; after 4 seasons most money wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Top three artists score 30/20/10 cumulatively; sell paintings to the bank; after 4 seasons most money wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Take turns as auctioneer, playing a painting with its auction type: open, once around, sealed, fixed price, or double.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has $100 behind a screen; deal painting cards (5 artists).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Only top-three artists of the season have value.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Sell high to others.
- Promote artists you own.
- Final-season paintings are worth most.

## Common mistakes

- Only top-three artists of the season have value.

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 45–45 min; budget about 55 min for a first game.
- Total: about 70 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Auction paintings using the method on each card: open, once-around, sealed, fixed price, double. The buyer pays the seller (or the bank if you buy your own). The 5th card of an artist ends the round. Rank artists: 30/20/10, cumulative. Sell to the bank; most cash after 4 rounds wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Read which artists others hold.
- **Mid:** Manipulate with doubles and sealed bids.
- **Late:** Concentrate money on top artists in round 4.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Is the 5th card auctioned?**  
  A: No.
- **Q: Are artists outside top 3 worth anything?**  
  A: Not this round.

## Overview

A tabletop MOBA: teams of heroes pick cards simultaneously and resolve by initiative; push minions, kill heroes, no dice.

## Setup

1. Two teams; map zones and minions; each hero has 5 cards.

## How to play

1. Everyone secretly plays 1 card; resolve by initiative.
2. Move, attack, use skills; level up to gain new cards.

## Game end & scoring

- Three ways to win: push the battle zone into the enemy throne; win the final push when the last wave token is used; or make the enemy spend their last life counter.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Three ways to win: push the battle zone into the enemy throne; win the final push when the last wave token is used; or make the enemy spend their last life counter.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Everyone secretly plays 1 card; resolve by initiative.
3. **Setup check:** Two teams; map zones and minions; each hero has 5 cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Best with 4 or 6 players.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Low initiative finishes, high initiative sets up.
- Retreat when low — deaths push the lane.
- Choose upgrades that complement teammates.

## Common mistakes

- Best with 4 or 6 players.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Overview

A storyteller gives a clue for a picture; others add matching pictures; everyone votes for the storyteller's card.

## Setup

1. Deal 6 picture cards each; rabbits on the score track; voting tokens.

## How to play

1. The storyteller picks a card and gives a clue (word, sentence, sound).
2. Others give a matching card face down; shuffle and reveal.
3. Everyone except the storyteller votes.

## Game end & scoring

- If all or nobody find the storyteller's card, others score 2 and the storyteller 0; otherwise storyteller and correct voters score 3; extra votes on your card score 1.
- Play to 30 points or until the deck runs out.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** If all or nobody find the storyteller's card, others score 2 and the storyteller 0; otherwise storyteller and correct voters score 3; extra votes on your card score 1.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The storyteller picks a card and gives a clue (word, sentence, sound).
3. **Setup check:** Deal 6 picture cards each; rabbits on the score track; voting tokens.
4. **The one rule people forget:** You cannot vote for your own card.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Clues should be partially obvious.
- Choose decoys that draw votes.
- Songs, poems and sounds work.

## Common mistakes

- You cannot vote for your own card.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 30–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 40 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Storyteller picks a card and gives a clue. Others give their best match; shuffle, reveal and vote. Some correct: storyteller and correct guessers +3, owners +1 per vote. All or none: storyteller 0, others +2.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Clues should be neither too obvious nor obscure.
- **Mid:** Match the clue's mood.
- **Late:** Maximise your storyteller turns late.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Does the storyteller vote?**  
  A: No.
- **Q: Can I vote for my own?**  
  A: No.

## Overview

Moving towers trap wizards: get all your wizards into the Raven Castle and fill all your potion bottles.

## Setup

1. Build the ring road; place 9 towers alternating raven/no-raven spaces.
2. Distribute wizards and bottles by player count; choose 2 spells; each player has 3 movement cards.

## How to play

1. Play a movement card: move your wizard exactly that many spaces clockwise, or move any tower (you may take just the top level).
2. Towers carry wizards inside them; buried wizards cannot move.
3. Landing on raven spaces lets you fill bottles; spells cost bottles.

## Game end & scoring

- Win by getting all wizards into the castle and filling all bottles.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win by getting all wizards into the castle and filling all bottles.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play a movement card: move your wizard exactly that many spaces clockwise, or move any tower (you may take just the top level).
3. **Setup check:** Build the ring road; place 9 towers alternating raven/no-raven spaces.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Moves must be exact.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Use towers to trap leading wizards.
- Moving a tower next to the castle can deliver wizards.
- Spend bottles on spells at key moments.

## Common mistakes

- Moves must be exact.
- Fill all bottles before your last wizard can enter.

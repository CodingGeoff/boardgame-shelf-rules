## Overview

Stack transparent part cards on transparent mech cards, then brawl with dice.

## Setup

1. Openly display mechs and parts.

## How to play

1. Draft: take turns picking (Zero+: 2 mechs + 4 parts each).
2. Assemble: overlay parts on right arm, left arm and back; stats combine visually.
3. Battle: attack in turns, rolling dice for hits and damage.

## Game end & scoring

- Destroy enemy mechs; in free-for-all, last standing or most kills wins (see rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Destroy enemy mechs; in free-for-all, last standing or most kills wins (see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draft: take turns picking (Zero+: 2 mechs + 4 parts each).
3. **Setup check:** Openly display mechs and parts.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Exact draft counts per rulebook.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Draft for your needs and to deny opponents.
- Arm and back combinations decide range and firepower.

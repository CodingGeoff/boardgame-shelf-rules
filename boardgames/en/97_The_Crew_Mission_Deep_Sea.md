## Overview

Cooperative trick-taking with task cards and limited communication across many missions.

## Setup

1. Deal all cards; the submarine 4 holder is captain.
2. Draw tasks to reach the mission's difficulty.

## How to play

1. Follow suit; submarines are trumps.
2. Each player may communicate once: show a card and mark it highest, lowest or only.

## Game end & scoring

- Complete all tasks to succeed and advance.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Complete all tasks to succeed and advance.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Follow suit; submarines are trumps.
3. **Setup check:** Deal all cards; the submarine 4 holder is captain.
4. **The one rule people forget:** No discussion of hands.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Use communication on key suits.
- Hold trumps for necessary tricks.
- Complete hardest tasks first.

## Common mistakes

- No discussion of hands.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

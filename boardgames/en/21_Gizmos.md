## Overview

Build machines powered by energy marbles and trigger chain reactions.

## Setup

1. Fill the energy dispenser with marbles and reveal 6 marbles below it.
2. Lay out machine cards in 3 tiers; each player starts with a basic machine.

## How to play

1. Choose one: Pick (take 1 revealed marble), File (archive a machine), Build (pay marbles to build a machine), or Research (look at cards and build/file one).
2. Built machines trigger on matching actions, creating chains.

## Game end & scoring

- Game ends when someone builds 16 machines or 4 level-III machines; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Game ends when someone builds 16 machines or 4 level-III machines; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Choose one: Pick (take 1 revealed marble), File (archive a machine), Build (pay marbles to build a machine), or Research (look at cards and build/file one).
3. **Setup check:** Fill the energy dispenser with marbles and reveal 6 marbles below it.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Each machine triggers at most once per turn.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Build triggers that chain into each other.
- Expand storage and archive capacity early.
- Plan level-III machines for big points.

## Common mistakes

- Each machine triggers at most once per turn.
- Energy storage limits apply.

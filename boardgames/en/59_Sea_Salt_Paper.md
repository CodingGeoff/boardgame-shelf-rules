## Overview

Collect origami sea creatures for sets, play pairs for effects, and end the round with Stop or Last Chance.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck; reveal two discard piles.

## How to play

1. Draw 2 keep 1 (discard the other), or take a discard top.
2. Optionally play duo pairs for effects (boat, fish, crab, swimmer + shark).
3. With 7+ points you may end the round: Stop, or Last Chance (others get one more turn; you bet you are highest).

## Game end & scoring

- Play rounds until the target score for the player count is reached.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Play rounds until the target score for the player count is reached.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw 2 keep 1 (discard the other), or take a discard top.
3. **Setup check:** Shuffle the deck; reveal two discard piles.
4. **The one rule people forget:** You need 7 points to end a round.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Mermaids and colour bonuses are late big points.
- Duos speed you up but reveal scores.
- Call Last Chance only when confident.

## Common mistakes

- You need 7 points to end a round.

## Overview

Monster brawl: roll Yahtzee-style dice to score, heal, gain energy or attack; holding Tokyo scores but takes hits.

## Setup

1. Choose monsters (10 health, 0 VP); reveal 3 power cards.

## How to play

1. Roll 6 dice up to 3 times.
2. Resolve: triples of numbers score; energy; claws attack (Tokyo vs outside); hearts heal (not in Tokyo).
3. Buy cards with energy; wickedness track (Dark Edition).

## Game end & scoring

- First to 20 VP or last monster standing wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to 20 VP or last monster standing wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Roll 6 dice up to 3 times.
3. **Setup check:** Choose monsters (10 health, 0 VP); reveal 3 power cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** You may leave Tokyo only when attacked.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Leave Tokyo when low.
- Energy cards give long-term power.
- Switch between VP and survival.

## Common mistakes

- You may leave Tokyo only when attacked.
- +2 VP for starting your turn in Tokyo.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

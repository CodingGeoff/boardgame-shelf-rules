## Overview

Wake the sleeping queens: kings wake, knights steal, potions put to sleep; arithmetic plays discard many cards.

## Setup

1. 12 queens face down in the centre; deal 5 cards.

## How to play

1. Play a king to wake a queen, a knight to steal (dragon blocks), a potion to put one to sleep (wand blocks).
2. Or discard a number, a pair, or an addition equation, then draw.

## Game end & scoring

- Win with 5 queens or 50 points (4–5 players: 4/40).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win with 5 queens or 50 points (4–5 players: 4/40).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play a king to wake a queen, a knight to steal (dragon blocks), a potion to put one to sleep (wand blocks).
3. **Setup check:** 12 queens face down in the centre; deal 5 cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Cat and Dog Queens cannot be held together.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Equations cycle bad cards.
- Keep a dragon or wand.
- The Rose Queen wakes another.

## Common mistakes

- Cat and Dog Queens cannot be held together.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Overview

Cooperative dungeon crawl with dexterity: bounce dice (behind your back, eyes closed…) onto a target to hit monsters.

## Setup

1. Place the target board; heroes with health; shared resources.

## How to play

1. Throw dice at the target per the monster's throwing rule.
2. Damage depends on the ring hit.
3. Shop for items between rooms.

## Game end & scoring

- Defeat the final boss to win; lose if all heroes fall.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Defeat the final boss to win; lose if all heroes fall.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Throw dice at the target per the monster's throwing rule.
3. **Setup check:** Place the target board; heroes with health; shared resources.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Throws off the target miss.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Practise a steady bounce.
- Save items for hard monsters.
- Let your best thrower lead.

## Common mistakes

- Throws off the target miss.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

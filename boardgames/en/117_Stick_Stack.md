## Overview

Dexterity balance game: draw a coloured stick and place it so every colour only touches the same colour on the tray or other sticks.

## Setup

1. Assemble base, pillar and tray; put the sticks in the bag.

## How to play

1. Draw 1 stick from the bag, or use one you collected from earlier falls.
2. Place it one-handed; at the moment it lands, each colour on the stick may only touch areas of the same colour.
3. Sticks that fall during your turn are yours.
4. Black edition: also follow a revealed instruction card (colour limit, which hand).

## Game end & scoring

- 2Plus quick rule: when the bag is empty, fewest sticks wins.
- English rules: round ends when all sticks are placed or the tower collapses; the toppler scores 5 points instead of collecting, others 1 point per stick; at 11+ the lowest score wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** 2Plus quick rule: when the bag is empty, fewest sticks wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draw 1 stick from the bag, or use one you collected from earlier falls.
3. **Setup check:** Assemble base, pillar and tray; put the sticks in the bag.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Box player counts vary (2–8, 2+, 2–12).
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Place toward the centre.
- Counterbalance the tray.
- Move slowly.

## Common mistakes

- Box player counts vary (2–8, 2+, 2–12).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

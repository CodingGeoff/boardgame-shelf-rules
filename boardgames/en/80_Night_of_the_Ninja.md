## Overview

Lotus and Crane clans assassinate each other: draft ninja cards, act in the night, then reveal.

## Setup

1. Take clan cards by player count; each player draws a secret clan card and 3 ninja cards.

## How to play

1. Draft: keep 1, pass 2; repeat.
2. Night: resolve in card number order (spy, mystic, trickster, assassin…).
3. Reveal and score.

## Game end & scoring

- Clan scores when the opponents' highest-ranked member is killed; the Ronin and others have own goals; play to the point target.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Clan scores when the opponents' highest-ranked member is killed; the Ronin and others have own goals; play to the point target.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draft: keep 1, pass 2; repeat.
3. **Setup check:** Take clan cards by player count; each player draws a secret clan card and 3 ninja cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Odd counts add the Ronin.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- What you pass is information.
- Reveal identities selectively.
- Assassinate the top-ranked enemy.

## Common mistakes

- Odd counts add the Ronin.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. 6p: secret clan cards, 3 ninja cards each.
2. Draft: keep 1 pass 2, keep 1 more.
3. Night: Spy phase I look at B; Tricksters swap.
4. Shinobi phase C checks me and kills me.
5. Reveal: compare the lowest surviving rank; Lotus wins and all Lotus members draw honour tokens.

### Scoring example

- Winning clan members draw hidden honour tokens; game ends at 10+.

### Rules FAQ / rulings

- **Q: Can dead players act?**  
  A: No, except reaction cards when killed.
- **Q: Can I show cards?**  
  A: No, but discussion is free.

### Player-count differences

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 15–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Lotus and Crane clans assassinate each other. Draft ninja cards, act at night in order: peek, swap, kill. Reveal at dawn.

### Strategy by phase

- **Mid:** Read identities at night.

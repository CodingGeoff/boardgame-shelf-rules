## Overview

3D Ubongo: when the timer starts, everyone builds a two-level shape with the given pieces; finishers take gems.

## Setup

1. Puzzle boards (3-piece and 4-piece sides) in stacks; each player has pieces.

## How to play

1. Roll or select pieces; flip the timer; fill your board exactly two levels high.
2. Call Ubongo when done; take gems in order.

## Game end & scoring

- After 9 rounds, most gems of one colour (or per rulebook) wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 9 rounds, most gems of one colour (or per rulebook) wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Roll or select pieces; flip the timer; fill your board exactly two levels high.
3. **Setup check:** Puzzle boards (3-piece and 4-piece sides) in stacks; each player has pieces.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Exactly two levels, no holes or overhang.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Place the largest or oddest piece first.
- Both levels must be complete.
- Start with 3-piece sides.

## Common mistakes

- Exactly two levels, no holes or overhang.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

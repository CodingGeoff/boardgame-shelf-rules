## Overview

Werewolf in one night: cards = players + 3; roles act in order and swap cards; then discuss and vote simultaneously.

## Setup

1. Use players + 3 role cards; 3 in the centre.

## How to play

1. Night in order: werewolves, minion, seer, robber, troublemaker, drunk, insomniac.
2. Day: discuss, then everyone points simultaneously.

## Game end & scoring

- Village wins if a werewolf dies; werewolves win if none die.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Village wins if a werewolf dies; werewolves win if none die.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Night in order: werewolves, minion, seer, robber, troublemaker, drunk, insomniac.
3. **Setup check:** Use players + 3 role cards; 3 in the centre.
4. **The one rule people forget:** No eliminations during the game.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Your card may have changed.
- Seer can view two centre cards.
- Wolves may claim troublemaker.

## Common mistakes

- No eliminations during the game.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

- **Q: What if no werewolves are among players?**

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 10–10 min; budget about 10 min for a first game.
- Total: about 15 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> One-night werewolf. Three extra role cards in the centre. Night actions in order: werewolves, seer, robber, troublemaker, drunk... At dawn discuss and vote simultaneously. Village wins by killing a werewolf; if no werewolves are among players, the village wins only if nobody dies.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Remember your night info.
- **Mid:** Share info and find contradictions.
- **Late:** Consider wolves in the centre.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: No werewolves among players?**  
  A: Village wins only if nobody dies (each gets one vote).
- **Q: Can my role change?**  
  A: Robber or Troublemaker may swap it; your final card counts.

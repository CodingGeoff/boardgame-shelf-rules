## Overview

With a standard deck, play cards face down in order A, 2, 3… K while claiming; anyone may call 'Cheat!'.

## Setup

1. Deal all cards (two decks for 5+ players).

## How to play

1. Play 1 or more cards face down claiming the next rank.
2. Anyone may call cheat: reveal. Lying: liar takes the pile; truthful: challenger takes it.

## Game end & scoring

- First to empty their hand wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to empty their hand wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play 1 or more cards face down claiming the next rank.
3. **Setup check:** Deal all cards (two decks for 5+ players).
4. **The one rule people forget:** The last card can be challenged too.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Mix a true card in when lying.
- Calling on a big pile is high risk.
- Count your own copies of that rank.

## Common mistakes

- The last card can be challenged too.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

## Overview

Classic icebreaker: the chosen player picks truth or dare.

## Setup

1. Choose a selection method.

## How to play

1. Selected player chooses truth or dare and draws a card.

## Game end & scoring

- No winner — play until the mood changes.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** No winner — play until the mood changes.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Selected player chooses truth or dare and draws a card.
3. **Setup check:** Choose a selection method.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Respect everyone's limits.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Agree boundaries first.
- Filter cards for the group.

## Common mistakes

- Respect everyone's limits.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

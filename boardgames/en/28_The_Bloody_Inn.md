## Overview

A grim 19th-century inn: recruit, bribe and kill travellers, bury bodies and launder money; richest wins.

## Setup

1. Set up the inn rooms and traveller deck; each player gets starting peasants and money.

## How to play

1. Each turn has two actions: bribe (recruit as accomplice), kill, bury, or build an annex.
2. Actions are paid by discarding accomplice cards of the required rank.
3. Fill rooms with travellers each round; police may check unburied bodies.

## Game end & scoring

- After the deck runs through twice, score money; richest wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After the deck runs through twice, score money; richest wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Each turn has two actions: bribe (recruit as accomplice), kill, bury, or build an annex.
3. **Setup check:** Set up the inn rooms and traveller deck; each player gets starting peasants and money.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Costs are paid by discarding accomplices.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Bribe low-rank accomplices early to afford actions.
- Annexes give burial space and ongoing effects.
- Do not hold unburied corpses when police arrive.

## Common mistakes

- Costs are paid by discarding accomplices.
- Unburied corpses risk penalties.

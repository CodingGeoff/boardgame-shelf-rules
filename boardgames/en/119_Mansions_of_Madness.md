## Overview

Cthulhu co-op adventure: the free app acts as keeper, generating maps, monsters and puzzles.

## Setup

1. Start the app, choose a scenario and investigators.

## How to play

1. Two actions per turn: move, search, interact, attack.
2. Horror checks reduce sanity; solve puzzles.
3. Mythos phase run by the app.

## Game end & scoring

- Scenario objectives revealed by the app.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Scenario objectives revealed by the app.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Two actions per turn: move, search, interact, attack.
3. **Setup check:** Start the app, choose a scenario and investigators.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The app is required.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Split up but avoid isolation.
- Sanity drains faster than health.
- Discuss clues together.

## Common mistakes

- The app is required.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together
- **Not ideal for:** when time is short

## Overview

Real-time puzzle race: roll to see which pieces to use and fill your board before the timer runs out.

## Setup

1. Each player has 12 pieces and puzzle cards (3- and 4-piece sides).

## How to play

1. Roll the die; check which pieces; flip the timer.
2. Fill the board exactly; call Ubongo; take gems in order.

## Game end & scoring

- After 9 rounds, most gems of one colour wins (per rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 9 rounds, most gems of one colour wins (per rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Roll the die; check which pieces; flip the timer.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has 12 pieces and puzzle cards (3- and 4-piece sides).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Exact fill only.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Largest piece first.
- Pieces may be flipped.
- Pick gem colours wisely.

## Common mistakes

- Exact fill only.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

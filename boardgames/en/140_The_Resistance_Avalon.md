## Overview

Loyal servants of Arthur vs minions of Mordred: leaders propose teams, everyone votes, quests succeed or fail; Merlin must stay hidden.

## Setup

1. Roles by player count; Merlin and evil see appropriate info.

## How to play

1. Leader proposes a team; everyone votes; 5 rejections = evil wins.
2. Team members secretly play success/fail.

## Game end & scoring

- 3 successes then the assassin guesses Merlin; 3 fails = evil wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** 3 successes then the assassin guesses Merlin; 3 fails = evil wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Leader proposes a team; everyone votes; 5 rejections = evil wins.
3. **Setup check:** Roles by player count; Merlin and evil see appropriate info.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Quest 4 needs two fails with 7+ players.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Merlin hints subtly.
- Evil sometimes passes.
- Percival protects Merlin.

## Common mistakes

- Quest 4 needs two fails with 7+ players.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 30–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 45 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Loyal knights versus Mordred's minions. The leader proposes a team; everyone votes. Approved team members secretly play success or fail — one fail sinks a quest (two on quest 4 with 7+). Three successes win for good unless the Assassin finds Merlin; three fails win for evil.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Good: track vote history.
- **Mid:** Merlin: hint subtly.
- **Late:** Evil: hunt Merlin at the end.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Five rejected votes?**  
  A: Evil wins.
- **Q: What does Percival see?**  
  A: Merlin and Morgana, indistinguishable.

## Overview

A revolver-themed bluffing and bidding game: hide the live round, spin, bid, challenge and pull the trigger to reach 15 VP.

## Setup

1. A revolver cylinder of 6 blanks + 1 live round (cards or tokens).
2. Each player or team has 4 lives; prepare VP tracking and bidding chips.

## How to play

1. Hide: the dealer secretly places the live round.
2. Spin: shuffle or rotate the cylinder; reveal partial info if the rules say so.
3. Bid: players bid how many trigger pulls or chips they dare.
4. Challenge: others may challenge the highest bidder.
5. Fire: the bidder pulls as bid. Blanks score VP; the live round costs a life.

## Game end & scoring

- First to 15 VP wins, or the last player or team alive.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to 15 VP wins, or the last player or team alive.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Hide: the dealer secretly places the live round.
3. **Setup check:** A revolver cylinder of 6 blanks + 1 live round (cards or tokens).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Details of spinning and challenge rewards vary — follow the rulebook.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Read the dealer: timing and expressions are information.
- Bid aggressively only when the odds favour you.
- In team mode coordinate bids and challenges.

## Common mistakes

- Details of spinning and challenge rewards vary — follow the rulebook.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

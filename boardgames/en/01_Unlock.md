## Overview

A real-time cooperative escape room in a deck of cards: reveal numbered cards, combine objects, enter codes in the free app, and escape within 60 minutes.

## Setup

1. Install the free Unlock! app and pick the scenario.
2. Place the start card face up; keep the deck numbered side down.
3. Start the 60-minute timer in the app.

## How to play

1. No turns: everyone plays at once and talks freely.
2. When a card shows a number or letter, find that card in the deck and reveal it.
3. Combine a blue object with a red object by adding their numbers, then reveal the card with the total.
4. Enter 4-digit codes (yellow cards) and machine actions in the app.
5. Hidden numbers can appear in the art; search every card carefully.
6. Wrong combinations or codes cost time penalties; the app also gives hints.

## Game end & scoring

- Escape before time runs out to win.
- The app gives a star rating based on time left, hints used and penalties.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Escape before time runs out to win.
2. **What you do on a turn:** No turns: everyone plays at once and talks freely.
3. **Setup check:** Install the free Unlock! app and pick the scenario.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Blue + red is addition only; grey cards combine with anything.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Lay out revealed cards so everyone can see them, and discard used ones.
- Announce every number you spot in the art — hidden numbers are the core puzzle.
- Use hints rather than guessing blindly; penalties cost more time.

## Common mistakes

- Blue + red is addition only; grey cards combine with anything.
- Do not flip cards just to peek; reveal only when a number points to them.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Overview

Third Azul: take tiles, build star-shaped pavilions with a rotating wild colour, aiming for big set bonuses.

## Setup

1. Each player gets a star board; place factory displays; the wild colour changes every round.

## How to play

1. Acquire: take all tiles of one colour from a factory (plus one wild if available).
2. Play: pay tiles of one colour (wilds can substitute) to place on a star space; score adjacency.
3. Keep up to 4 tiles between rounds.

## Game end & scoring

- After 6 rounds, score completed stars, numbers and bonuses; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 6 rounds, score completed stars, numbers and bonuses; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Acquire: take all tiles of one colour from a factory (plus one wild if available).
3. **Setup check:** Each player gets a star board; place factory displays; the wild colour changes every round.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The current round's wild colour cannot be taken as a regular tile with others.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Track the wild colour order to plan.
- Cover bonus spaces around pillars, statues and windows.
- Avoid taking too many tiles to discard.

## Common mistakes

- The current round's wild colour cannot be taken as a regular tile with others.
- Excess tiles above 4 cost points.

## Overview

Detective vs. phantom thief: the thief disguises as one of 6 suspects to open the real chest; the detective must expose him.

## Setup

1. The detective hides treasure in 3 chests; the thief secretly picks a disguise.

## How to play

1. Players alternately draw and place cards into 6 rows.
2. When a row reaches 5 cards it resolves.
3. Track which rows the thief pushes.

## Game end & scoring

- Thief wins by opening the real chest in disguise; detective wins by identifying him or when decks run out (see rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Thief wins by opening the real chest in disguise; detective wins by identifying him or when decks run out (see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Players alternately draw and place cards into 6 rows.
3. **Setup check:** The detective hides treasure in 3 chests; the thief secretly picks a disguise.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Rows resolve at 5 cards.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Thief: push decoy rows.
- Detective: log pushed rows and eliminate suspects.
- Accuse only when sure.

## Common mistakes

- Rows resolve at 5 cards.
- Exact accusation penalties per rulebook.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 20–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 45 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> One player is the detective, one the phantom thief. The thief secretly disguises as one of six suspects and tries to open the real treasure chest; the detective watches movements to identify the thief. Thief wins with the treasure; detective wins by catching the right suspect.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Thief: make suspects behave alike; don't rush the chest.
- **Mid:** Detective: track each suspect's path and eliminate.
- **Late:** Thief sprints late; detective accuses only when confident.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: How many accusations?**  
  A: Limited; see rulebook.
- **Q: Are some chests fake?**  
  A: Yes, the thief needs the real one.

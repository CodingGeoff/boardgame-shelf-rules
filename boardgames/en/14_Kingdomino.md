## Overview

Connect domino-like terrain tiles to form a 5×5 kingdom; each region scores tiles × crowns.

## Setup

1. Each player has a starting castle tile.
2. Lay out a row of dominoes sorted by number; place kings on them to choose.

## How to play

1. The order of kings on the current row determines turn order.
2. Add your chosen domino to your kingdom: at least one side must match terrain or touch the castle.
3. Place your king on a domino in the next row (lower numbers go first next round).

## Game end & scoring

- Score each connected region: number of tiles × number of crowns in it.
- Optional bonuses: castle in the centre, complete 5×5.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Score each connected region: number of tiles × number of crowns in it.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The order of kings on the current row determines turn order.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has a starting castle tile.
4. **The one rule people forget:** A tile that cannot be placed is discarded.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Taking a high-numbered tile means picking later next round.
- Crowns are rare — combine them with large regions.
- Keep the 5×5 boundary in mind.

## Common mistakes

- A tile that cannot be placed is discarded.
- The kingdom must fit inside 5×5 (7×7 in 2-player variant).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

### Component checklist

From the Chinese doc (original wording):

- [ ] 48 块多米诺板块**：每块分两半，各是一种地形（麦田、森林、湖泊、草原、沼泽、矿山），部分格子带**王冠**。正面是地形，背面是编号 1–48（编号越大越值钱）。
- [ ] 4 个起始方块、4 座 3D 城堡
- [ ] 8 个国王（4 色 × 2）

Before you start:
- Count the components against the rulebook list; sort cards by type — a missing card can break the balance.
- Check tokens, dice and score markers; coins or generic tokens can stand in for missing pieces.
- Hand out reference cards before the first game to save rulebook time.

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Reveal dominoes equal to players, sorted by number.
2. I took the best tile last round, so I pick last now.
3. I place a forest-forest domino next to my forest.
4. The kingdom must fit 5×5; unplaceable tiles are discarded.

### Scoring example

- Each area scores squares × crowns. E.g. 6 forest squares with 2 crowns = 12.

### Rules FAQ / rulings

- **Q: Areas without crowns?**  
  A: Score 0.

### Player-count differences

- 2–4; 7×7 'Mighty Duel' at 2.
- Best with: 2 players.

### Official variants & expansions

- Optional: centred castle +10, complete 5×5 +5. Age of Giants expansion.

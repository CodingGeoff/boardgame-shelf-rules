## Overview

Two secret societies on a carriage: the Order needs 3 keys, the Brotherhood 3 goblets; trade and attack to identify allies and declare victory.

## Setup

1. Deal equal faction cards plus profession and character cards.
2. Deal items (players − 2 + 2 secret bags), 1 each.

## How to play

1. On your turn: trade or attack.
2. In attacks, others may support either side.
3. Winning attacker takes an item or views a faction (see rulebook); ties let the attacker draw an item.

## Game end & scoring

- Declare victory on your turn by naming all teammates and meeting the item condition; a wrong declaration loses.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Declare victory on your turn by naming all teammates and meeting the item condition; a wrong declaration loses.
2. **What you do on a turn:** On your turn: trade or attack.
3. **Setup check:** Deal equal faction cards plus profession and character cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Odd player counts adjust factions.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Trades reveal a lot — note what people refuse.
- Secret bags enable bluffs.
- Confirm teammates before declaring.

## Common mistakes

- Odd player counts adjust factions.
- A failed declaration loses immediately.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 30–60 min; budget about 70 min for a first game.
- Total: about 85 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Passengers belong to two hidden factions: the Order wants 3 keys, the Brotherhood 3 goblets. On your turn trade an item or attack. Use trades and fights to find teammates. Declare victory when you think your side qualifies — a wrong declaration loses.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Probe with trades — those passing keys may be allies.
- **Mid:** Attacks reveal items and identity hints.
- **Late:** Declare as soon as you're sure your team qualifies.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: What's needed to declare?**  
  A: Your team collectively holds 3 matching items (see rulebook).
- **Q: Odd player counts?**  
  A: A neutral Hermit is used; see rulebook.

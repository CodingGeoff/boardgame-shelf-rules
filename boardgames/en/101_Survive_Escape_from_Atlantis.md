## Overview

The island sinks; rescue your people to safety while sharks, sea serpents and whales hunt others.

## Setup

1. Build the island (beach, forest, mountain); place people (hidden values) and boats.

## How to play

1. Play a held tile; move 3 spaces; remove a land tile (beach → forest → mountain); roll the creature die and move one.

## Game end & scoring

- When the volcano appears, the game ends; score rescued people's hidden values.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When the volcano appears, the game ends; score rescued people's hidden values.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play a held tile; move 3 spaces; remove a land tile (beach → forest → mountain); roll the creature die and move one.
3. **Setup check:** Build the island (beach, forest, mountain); place people (hidden values) and boats.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Remove tiles outside-in.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Evacuate high-value people first.
- Removed tiles may give immediate or held effects.
- Target full boats.

## Common mistakes

- Remove tiles outside-in.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** most board gamers
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 45–60 min; budget about 70 min for a first game.
- Total: about 80 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> The island sinks — get your people to safety. Move people and boats, remove a tile (with effects), then roll to move sharks, sea serpents or whales to harass others. Score the values on rescued people.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Move high-value people toward the edge.
- **Mid:** Use boats for multiple people.
- **Late:** Send monsters at the leader.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Can I see people values?**  
  A: Only your own.
- **Q: Can serpents sink boats?**  
  A: Yes.

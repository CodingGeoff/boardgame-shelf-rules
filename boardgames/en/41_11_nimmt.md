## Overview

A sibling of 6 nimmt!: add cards to piles; if you cannot, take a whole pile; taking big piles earns bull cards for extra plays.

## Setup

1. Deal hands; start the piles per rulebook.

## How to play

1. Play 1 card onto a pile top (placement condition per rulebook).
2. If you cannot play, you must take a whole pile; you may also take one voluntarily.
3. Taking more than 3 cards earns a bull card, which lets you play extra cards on a turn.
4. When bull cards run out, take one from the player with the most.

## Game end & scoring

- Score bullheads on taken cards; fewest wins (see rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Score bullheads on taken cards; fewest wins (see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play 1 card onto a pile top (placement condition per rulebook).
3. **Setup check:** Deal hands; start the piles per rulebook.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Different rules from 6 nimmt! — do not mix them up.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Voluntarily take small piles to gain tempo and bulls.
- Use bull cards to empty your hand quickly.
- Track pile tops to plan sequences.

## Common mistakes

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 20–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Play a card onto a pile if it fits; if not, take an entire pile. When you collect more than 3 cards you may use a bull card to gain extra plays. Fewest bull heads wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Play cards that fit easily.
- **Mid:** When forced, take the cheapest pile.
- **Late:** Use bull cards for extra plays late.

## Overview

One guesser, one honest explainer and everyone else bluffs definitions of strange words.

## Setup

1. Everyone starts with 3 points; the guesser takes 2 special cards; word cards black side up.

## How to play

1. Everyone closes eyes for 9 seconds; the honest player peeks at the true meaning.
2. The guesser reads the word; everyone explains it (bluffers invent).
3. The guesser asks follow-ups and picks the honest player.

## Game end & scoring

- Correct pick: guesser and honest player both gain the word's difficulty (1–3).
- Picking a bluffer gives only that bluffer the difficulty points.
- 'Gong San Xiao' cards (guesser has 2, used during follow-ups): on a bluffer, bluffer −1 and guesser +1; on the honest player, guesser −3.
- Everyone starts with 3 points; the guesser role passes left.
- Play to the agreed score or rounds.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Correct pick: guesser and honest player both gain the word's difficulty (1–3).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Everyone closes eyes for 9 seconds; the honest player peeks at the true meaning.
3. **Setup check:** Everyone starts with 3 points; the guesser takes 2 special cards; word cards black side up.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Scoring per rulebook.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Bluff like a dictionary: etymology then usage.
- The honest player can sound flat to seem credible.
- Guessers probe details — fakes contradict themselves.

## Common mistakes

- Scoring per rulebook.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

## Overview

A legacy council game: vote on dilemmas, power and coins flow, and the story branches over 15+ sessions.

## Setup

1. Set up the house boards, agendas and the kingdom board.

## How to play

1. Read the dilemma (yes/no).
2. Vote in order: aye, nay, or abstain (regain power/coins).
3. Resolve outcomes, stickers and envelopes.

## Game end & scoring

- Each session ends by its conditions; score per agenda; the campaign continues.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Each session ends by its conditions; score per agenda; the campaign continues.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Read the dilemma (yes/no).
3. **Setup check:** Set up the house boards, agendas and the kingdom board.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Legacy changes are permanent.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Abstaining builds resources.
- Secret agendas decide scoring.
- Do not spend all power in one session.

## Common mistakes

- Legacy changes are permanent.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 60–60 min; budget about 70 min for a first game.
- Total: about 85 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> A legacy kingdom council game. Vote on dilemma cards; power and coin flow. Story branches across sessions and permanently change the kingdom.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Learn your secret agenda.
- **Mid:** Ally for key votes.
- **Late:** Build power for the legacy.

## Overview

A two-act cooperative dungeon crawl: explore and fight to gather loot, then destroy the altar and defeat the boss before the torch burns out.

## Setup

1. Place the entrance tile; heroes start there.
2. Choose heroes: 2 for solo, 1 each in multiplayer (see rulebook).
3. Set the torch marker at the start of the torch track.

## How to play

1. Each hero turn = move + one action (fight, explore, heal, rest, use ability, trade).
2. Exploring unknown exits draws new room tiles.
3. Dungeon turn: the torch drops, monsters spawn and attack.
4. Defeating minions gives loot and raises the torch by 6.

## Game end & scoring

- Act II: the boss appears with altars; destroy altars and defeat the boss to win.
- Lose if heroes fall or the torch runs out.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Act II: the boss appears with altars; destroy altars and defeat the boss to win.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Each hero turn = move + one action (fight, explore, heal, rest, use ability, trade).
3. **Setup check:** Place the entrance tile; heroes start there.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The torch drops every round.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- The torch is time — prioritise killing minions to relight it.
- Split roles: tank absorbs, damage dealers clear, support manages mana.
- Gear up before Act II.

## Common mistakes

- The torch drops every round.
- Solo play controls 2 heroes.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

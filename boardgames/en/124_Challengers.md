## Overview

A board game 'auto-battler': draft cards into your deck, battle automatically on a schedule, collect trophies, and reach the final.

## Setup

1. Choose themed sets; each player has a starter deck.

## How to play

1. Draft cards, adjust your deck, then duel: flip cards comparing power; beaten cards go to the bench; a full bench loses.

## Game end & scoring

- After 7 rounds, top two play the final.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 7 rounds, top two play the final.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Draft cards, adjust your deck, then duel: flip cards comparing power; beaten cards go to the bench; a full bench loses.
3. **Setup check:** Choose themed sets; each player has a starter deck.
4. **The one rule people forget:** 45 minutes regardless of player count.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

- Theme synergies.
- Lean decks draw key cards.
- Watch bench limits.

### Component checklist

- [ ] 75 种角色卡，分为 6 个主题套组，每局挑选套组使用。
- [ ] A / B / C 三个卡堆，后面的更强。

Before you start:
- Count the components against the rulebook list; sort cards by type — a missing card can break the balance.
- Check tokens, dice and score markers; coins or generic tokens can stand in for missing pieces.
- Hand out reference cards before the first game to save rulebook time.

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Draft cards each round.
2. Matches: flip and compare power; the losing side flips.
3. My strong card overtakes and his card goes to the bench.
4. His bench fills: I win the trophy.
5. After 7 rounds the top two play the final.

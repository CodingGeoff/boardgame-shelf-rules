## Overview

Explore a haunted house; at some point the Haunt begins, one player becomes the traitor, and both sides follow a secret scenario.

## Setup

1. Choose explorers (4 traits); place starting tiles.

## How to play

1. Move by speed, discover rooms, draw events, items or omens.
2. After each omen, roll a haunt check.
3. When the haunt starts, look up the scenario; the traitor leaves to read their book.

## Game end & scoring

- Each scenario defines win conditions for heroes and traitor.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Each scenario defines win conditions for heroes and traitor.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Move by speed, discover rooms, draw events, items or omens.
3. **Setup check:** Choose explorers (4 traits); place starting tiles.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Haunt rolls grow with omens.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Collect items before the haunt.
- Traitors learn rules quickly; heroes plan together.
- Enjoy the story.

## Common mistakes

- Haunt rolls grow with omens.
- Scenario numbers differ by edition.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** most board gamers
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

- Hand out reference cards before the first game to save rulebook time.

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Explorers start in the entrance hall; I open a door and draw a room.
2. An omen symbol: draw an omen and roll 6 dice for the haunt.
3. The roll is under the omen count — the haunt begins.
4. Look up the haunt and traitor; the traitor leaves to read their book.
5. Each side pursues their goal.

### Scoring example

- Win per the haunt scenario.

### Rules FAQ / rulings

- **Q: How many dice for haunt rolls?**  
  A: Six dice; trigger if below the omen count.

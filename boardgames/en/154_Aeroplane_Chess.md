## Overview

Chinese classic dice race: roll a 6 to take off, jump on own colour, fly shortcuts and bump opponents home.

## Setup

1. Four planes per player in the hangar.

## How to play

1. Roll 6 to launch; move by die.
2. Landing on your colour jumps ahead; flight lines shortcut.
3. Landing on opponents sends them home.

## Game end & scoring

- First to bring all 4 planes home wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to bring all 4 planes home wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Roll 6 to launch; move by die.
3. **Setup check:** Four planes per player in the hangar.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Home usually needs an exact roll.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Launch several planes.
- Stay out of 1–6 range behind opponents.
- Agree house rules first.

## Common mistakes

- Home usually needs an exact roll.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** families with young children
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 20–40 min; budget about 50 min for a first game.
- Total: about 55 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Ludo-style race. Roll a 6 to launch (some allow 5–6). Land on your colour to jump ahead; dashed lines let you fly across. Landing on an opponent sends them home. First to bring all 4 planes home wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Launch several planes.
- **Mid:** Bump opponents.
- **Late:** Exact rolls to finish.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Need a 6 to launch?**  
  A: Usually 6; some allow 5.
- **Q: Roll again on 6?**  
  A: Usually yes.

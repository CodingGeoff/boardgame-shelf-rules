## Overview

Flip character cards; only when a character performs their own job's action does everyone react; slowest or wrong takes the pile.

## Setup

1. Put badges (players − 1) in the centre; deal the rest face down.

## How to play

1. Flip your top card outward to the centre.
2. Policeman with badge: grab a badge. Judge with gavel: slap and shout 'Guilty!'. Banker with money: hand on the card. Thief with jewels: hands up.
3. Non-matching combinations: stay still; reacting is a mistake.

## Game end & scoring

- Slowest or wrong player takes the face-up cards; first to empty their deck wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Slowest or wrong player takes the face-up cards; first to empty their deck wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Flip your top card outward to the centre.
3. **Setup check:** Put badges (players − 1) in the centre; deal the rest face down.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Flip outward so you do not see first.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Match character and prop.
- Keep hands near the badges.
- Do not mirror other people's mistakes.

## Common mistakes

- Flip outward so you do not see first.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

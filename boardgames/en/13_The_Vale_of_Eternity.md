## Overview

A monster-summoning drafting engine: hunt, summon and chain effects over 10 rounds to reach 60 points.

## Setup

1. Each player gets a board and a few stones (magic currency).
2. Lay out the card market for the hunting phase.

## How to play

1. Hunting: players take turns drafting market cards; unpicked cards may be sold for stones.
2. Action: sell cards for stones, tame cards into hand, or summon cards to your area by paying stones.
3. Summon limit rises each round; effects can trigger immediately, permanently or at round end.

## Game end & scoring

- Game ends when someone reaches 60 points or after round 10; highest score wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Game ends when someone reaches 60 points or after round 10; highest score wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Hunting: players take turns drafting market cards; unpicked cards may be sold for stones.
3. **Setup check:** Each player gets a board and a few stones (magic currency).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Stones have values 1, 3 and 6 and there is a holding limit.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Build combos across the five families (fire, water, earth, wind, dragon).
- Stone management is tight; selling at the right time matters.
- Watch the summon limit each round.

## Common mistakes

- Stones have values 1, 3 and 6 and there is a holding limit.
- Summon limit is per round.

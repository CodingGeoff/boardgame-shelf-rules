## Overview

A Wild West train robbery: everyone programmes action cards in turn, then they resolve in order — chaos ensues.

## Setup

1. Build the 3D train (locomotive + carriages = players); place loot and the marshal.

## How to play

1. Schemin': play cards to a common pile per round card (face up, face down, double).
2. Stealin': resolve in order — move, change floor, shoot, punch, rob, move marshal.

## Game end & scoring

- After 5 rounds, most money wins; the best shooter gets a bonus.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 5 rounds, most money wins; the best shooter gets a bonus.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Schemin': play cards to a common pile per round card (face up, face down, double).
3. **Setup check:** Build the 3D train (locomotive + carriages = players); place loot and the marshal.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Hidden cards stay hidden until resolution.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Remember opponents' programmed moves.
- Bullets clog hands; draw to recover.
- Use the marshal to guard loot.

## Common mistakes

- Hidden cards stay hidden until resolution.

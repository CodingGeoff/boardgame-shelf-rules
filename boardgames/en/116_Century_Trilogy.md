## Overview

Spice Road: use merchant cards to produce, upgrade and trade four spices and claim point cards; the sequels stand alone or combine.

## Setup

1. Spices: yellow < red < green < brown; caravan limit 10.
2. 6 merchant cards and 5 point cards (gold and silver coins on the first two); starting hand of 2.

## How to play

1. Choose one: play a card, acquire a merchant (pay spices to skip cards), claim a point card, or rest (return played cards).

## Game end & scoring

- Game ends when someone claims their 5th (4–5p) or 6th point card; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Game ends when someone claims their 5th (4–5p) or 6th point card; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Choose one: play a card, acquire a merchant (pay spices to skip cards), claim a point card, or rest (return played cards).
3. **Setup check:** Spices: yellow < red < green < brown; caravan limit 10.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Discard spices over 10.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Build your engine early.
- Coins reward early claims.
- Time your rests.

## Common mistakes

- Discard spices over 10.

## Overview

F1 racing with gearbox dice: higher gears roll bigger dice; you must stop the required number of times in corners or lose tyres and brakes.

## Setup

1. Choose the track; set wear points per car.

## How to play

1. Shift up one gear or down (skipping gears costs wear); roll that gear's die and move exactly.
2. Corners require stops; overshooting costs tyre points (too much eliminates).
3. Blocking, collisions and failures per rules.

## Game end & scoring

- First across the finish after the laps wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First across the finish after the laps wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Shift up one gear or down (skipping gears costs wear); roll that gear's die and move exactly.
3. **Setup check:** Choose the track; set wear points per car.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Overshooting corners costs tyres; 0 = out.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Downshift before corners.
- Plan to land just inside corner stops.
- Spend wear on the last lap.

## Common mistakes

- Overshooting corners costs tyres; 0 = out.

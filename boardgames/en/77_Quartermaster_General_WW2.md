## Overview

Axis vs Allies: 6 nations with separate decks build armies and fight using only cards; cut supply and units disappear.

## Setup

1. Teams: Germany, Japan, Italy vs UK, USSR, USA; each nation has its own deck.

## How to play

1. Each nation turn: play 1 card (build army/navy, land/sea battle, event, status), check supply, score.
2. Units must trace supply to the capital.

## Game end & scoring

- After the last round or when a team reaches the threshold, highest team score wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After the last round or when a team reaches the threshold, highest team score wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Each nation turn: play 1 card (build army/navy, land/sea battle, event, status), check supply, score.
3. **Setup check:** Teams: Germany, Japan, Italy vs UK, USSR, USA; each nation has its own deck.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Never mix nation decks.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Coordinate nation sequencing.
- Cutting supply beats direct attacks.
- Status cards early.

## Common mistakes

- Never mix nation decks.

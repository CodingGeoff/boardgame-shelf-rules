## Overview

Two markers tug-of-war on a power track; each leader serves two factions; end conditions are checked in priority order.

## Setup

1. Deal each player a secret leader (two allegiances); markers start at 4; deal hero hands.

## How to play

1. Play a hero face up (use its ability) or face down (hide its faction).
2. Refill per rules.

## Game end & scoring

- Check in order: Undead (both in Dark War zone), Water Folk (adjacent/same), Empire (red ahead by 2+), Hill Clans (green ahead by 2+).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Check in order: Undead (both in Dark War zone), Water Folk (adjacent/same), Empire (red ahead by 2+), Hill Clans (green ahead by 2+).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play a hero face up (use its ability) or face down (hide its faction).
3. **Setup check:** Deal each player a secret leader (two allegiances); markers start at 4; deal hero hands.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The order of checks matters.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Pick the easier of your two paths.
- Read others' pushes.
- Face-down plays help in tiebreaks.

## Common mistakes

- The order of checks matters.

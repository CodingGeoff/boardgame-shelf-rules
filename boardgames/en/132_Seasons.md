## Overview

Three years of 12 seasons: draft ability cards and split them across years, then pick seasonal dice to summon items and familiars for crystals.

## Setup

1. Prelude: draft 9 cards and split into 3 years of 3.

## How to play

1. Each season roll dice; players pick one (energy, crystals, summon slots, draw).
2. Summon cards with energy; transmute energy to crystals (rate depends on season).

## Game end & scoring

- After 3 years, score crystals and card points; bonus cards cost points.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** After 3 years, score crystals and card points; bonus cards cost points.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Each season roll dice; players pick one (energy, crystals, summon slots, draw).
3. **Setup check:** Prelude: draft 9 cards and split into 3 years of 3.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Likely a bootleg edition; component quality varies.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Plan combos across years.
- Raise summon slots.
- Watch time-moving dice.

## Common mistakes

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 15 min (weight ●●●).
- Play: 60–60 min; budget about 70 min for a first game.
- Total: about 85 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Three years, twelve seasons. Draft 9 power cards and split them across the years. Each turn take a season die for energy, crystals, summoning, cards. Summon items and familiars. Score crystals + cards − penalties.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Plan your three-year split in the draft.
- **Mid:** Exploit season changes.
- **Late:** Push crystals in year 3.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Season dice?**  
  A: First player rolls; pick in turn.
- **Q: Unused cards penalised?**  
  A: Yes.

## Overview

Charades telephone with roles: the head goose sees the case and passes it on with gestures and honks; a bad goose sabotages.

## Setup

1. Roles: head goose, bad goose, good geese.

## How to play

1. Head goose views the culprit and victim cards.
2. Pass by gestures and honks only.
3. Guess from the last player backwards; then vote for the bad goose.

## Game end & scoring

- Head and good geese win if the case is guessed and the bad goose is caught.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Head and good geese win if the case is guessed and the bad goose is caught.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Head goose views the culprit and victim cards.
3. **Setup check:** Roles: head goose, bad goose, good geese.
4. **The one rule people forget:** No speaking.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Keep gestures simple.
- Bad goose distorts subtly.
- Remember each gesture.

## Common mistakes

- No speaking.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

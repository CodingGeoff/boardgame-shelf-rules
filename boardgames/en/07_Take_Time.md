## Overview

A silent cooperative campaign puzzle (Libellud): place 12 face-down sun/moon number cards around the 6 sectors of a clock so sector sums never decrease clockwise and never exceed 24.

## Setup

1. Components: 24 number cards (12 white sun cards 1–12, 12 black moon cards 1–12), clock hand, 3 reminder and 3 bonus tokens, 10 chapter envelopes of 4 clocks each (40 levels), a Regrets envelope and a Rebirth envelope.
2. Reveal the level's clock, shuffle sun and moon cards and deal 12 cards among players (distribution by player count per rulebook); place reminder/bonus tokens as instructed.

## How to play

1. Discussion phase: read the level's rules and discuss strategy freely BEFORE looking at cards — no codes and no revealing values.
2. Placement phase (silence): pick up your cards and place them face down next to the sectors until all 12 are placed. Tokens may be used under their conditions.
3. General rules: every sector gets at least 1 card; starting from the marked sector and going clockwise, sector sums must be increasing OR EQUAL; no sector sum may exceed 24.
4. Resolution: flip clockwise from the hand and check every rule. Success advances; failure can be retried or the level placed in the Regrets envelope.

## Game end & scoring

- Each chapter has 4 levels (10 chapters, 40 levels); new symbols and rules are added as you progress.
- After all levels, open the Rebirth envelope for the final challenge.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Each chapter has 4 levels (10 chapters, 40 levels); new symbols and rules are added as you progress.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Discussion phase: read the level's rules and discuss strategy freely BEFORE looking at cards — no codes and no revealing values.
3. **Setup check:** Components: 24 number cards (12 white sun cards 1–12, 12 black moon cards 1–12), clock hand, 3 reminder and 3 bonus tokens, 10 chapter envelopes of 4 clocks each (40 levels), a Regrets envelope and a Rebirth envelope.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Equal sums are allowed; decreasing is not.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Agree an overall shape (small numbers early, big later) and who should play first.
- Hesitation and play order carry information.
- Debriefing after a failure is part of the fun.

## Common mistakes

- Equal sums are allowed; decreasing is not.
- No talking once cards are picked up.
- Each sector needs at least one card.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

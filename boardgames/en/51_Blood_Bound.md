## Overview

Two vampire clans hunt each other's leader: the dagger holder attacks, the wounded reveals one icon, and the 4th wound ends the game.

## Setup

1. Each player takes a character (clan, rank 1–9, 3 icons, clue); show your clue to the left neighbour.
2. Add the Inquisitor with odd player counts.

## How to play

1. The dagger holder attacks a player (or passes per rules).
2. Others may intervene to take the wound.
3. The wounded chooses an icon to reveal (clan colours or rank); revealing rank may trigger an ability.
4. The dagger goes to the wounded player.

## Game end & scoring

- The 4th wound captures a player: capturing the enemy leader wins for your clan; capturing a non-leader loses.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** The 4th wound captures a player: capturing the enemy leader wins for your clan; capturing a non-leader loses.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The dagger holder attacks a player (or passes per rules).
3. **Setup check:** Each player takes a character (clan, rank 1–9, 3 icons, clue); show your clue to the left neighbour.
4. **The one rule people forget:** The leader is the lowest rank in each clan.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Revealing the other clan's colour misleads.
- Intervening protects the leader but shows allegiance.
- Be sure before capturing.

## Common mistakes

- The leader is the lowest rank in each clan.
- The Inquisitor has separate goals.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 20–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 45 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Two vampire clans hunt each other's leader. Each player has a rank and clan. The dagger holder attacks; the wounded reveals a clan or rank symbol. A fourth wound means capture. Capture the enemy leader to win.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Attack suspects to see what they reveal.
- **Mid:** Use powers to protect or mislead.
- **Late:** Focus the leader once identified.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Can I attack my own clan?**  
  A: Yes, though rarely wise.
- **Q: How does intervention work?**  
  A: Others can take the wound instead; see rulebook.

## Overview

Four heists: play number cards to take or steal loot, but whoever has the fewest alibis is caught.

## Setup

1. Split loot into 4 sets; place the watchdog in the centre; deal 5 cards each.

## How to play

1. Play 1 card and resolve, then refill to 5.
2. Number cards (0–5): take loot of that value from the centre; if none, steal it from a player.
3. Watchdog: take the watchdog token, which protects from theft.
4. Greedy Thief: take any loot from the centre (cannot steal).
5. Boss: take the boss disc.

## Game end & scoring

- A raid ends when the centre is empty: keep the boss disc only if you also took a 4 or 5 of that series; stack loot face down (now safe).
- After 4 raids, the player with fewest alibis is arrested; highest score among the rest wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** A raid ends when the centre is empty: keep the boss disc only if you also took a 4 or 5 of that series; stack loot face down (now safe).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play 1 card and resolve, then refill to 5.
3. **Setup check:** Split loot into 4 sets; place the watchdog in the centre; deal 5 cards each.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Greedy Thief cannot steal from players.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Steal high loot near the end of a raid.
- Take the watchdog when you hold valuable loot.
- Do not neglect alibis.

## Common mistakes

- Greedy Thief cannot steal from players.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 20–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 35 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Four rounds of dividing loot. Play number cards to take or steal loot; bigger numbers act first. But whoever has the fewest alibis at the end is arrested. Most loot wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Secure alibis first.
- **Mid:** Use high numbers for key loot or theft.
- **Late:** Count alibis carefully in the final round.

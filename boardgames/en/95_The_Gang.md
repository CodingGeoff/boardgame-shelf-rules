## Overview

Cooperative Texas Hold'em: no talking about cards — take chips to signal your hand strength; the team must rank correctly.

## Setup

1. Deal 2 hole cards; four chip colours numbered 1 to players.

## How to play

1. Each betting round (pre-flop, flop, turn, river) take a chip of that colour expressing your rank; you may steal others' chips.
2. At showdown, check whether ranks match chip order.

## Game end & scoring

- 3 successful heists win; 3 failures lose.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** 3 successful heists win; 3 failures lose.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Each betting round (pre-flop, flop, turn, river) take a chip of that colour expressing your rank; you may steal others' chips.
3. **Setup check:** Deal 2 hole cards; four chip colours numbered 1 to players.
4. **The one rule people forget:** No verbal or facial signals.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Taking a chip is communication.
- Read community cards.
- Start cautious, refine later.

## Common mistakes

- No verbal or facial signals.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together; people who love deduction and guessing identities; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 10 min (weight ●●).
- Play: 20–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 35 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Co-op Texas Hold'em. No talking about cards — take star chips to express your hand's strength. Re-take chips after each community reveal. At showdown, if rankings match, you crack a vault. 3 vaults win; 3 alarms lose.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Take chips based on hole cards first.
- **Mid:** Watch others' hesitation.
- **Late:** Swap chips carefully at the end.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Can I take someone's chip?**  
  A: Yes.
- **Q: Can I say my hand is good?**  
  A: No.

# Unlock! (Escape Room Card Series)

> Chinese name: **大搜查!（Unlock!）** · Doc #1 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../01_%E5%A4%A7%E6%90%9C%E6%9F%A5_Unlock%E5%AF%86%E5%AE%A4%E9%80%83%E8%84%B1.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/1)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 1–6 (best: 2-4) |
| Play time | 60 min |
| Age | 10+ |
| Complexity | ●●○○○ Light (2/5) |
| Tags | co-op, puzzle-solving, escape room, cards, app |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg)
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 左侧整摞

## Overview

A real-time cooperative escape room in a deck of cards: reveal numbered cards, combine objects, enter codes in the free app, and escape within 60 minutes.

## Setup

1. Install the free Unlock! app and pick the scenario.
2. Place the start card face up; keep the deck numbered side down.
3. Start the 60-minute timer in the app.

## How to play

1. No turns: everyone plays at once and talks freely.
2. When a card shows a number or letter, find that card in the deck and reveal it.
3. Combine a blue object with a red object by adding their numbers, then reveal the card with the total.
4. Enter 4-digit codes (yellow cards) and machine actions in the app.
5. Hidden numbers can appear in the art; search every card carefully.
6. Wrong combinations or codes cost time penalties; the app also gives hints.

## Game end & scoring

- Escape before time runs out to win.
- The app gives a star rating based on time left, hints used and penalties.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Escape before time runs out to win.
2. **What you do on a turn:** No turns: everyone plays at once and talks freely.
3. **Setup check:** Install the free Unlock! app and pick the scenario.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Blue + red is addition only; grey cards combine with anything.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Lay out revealed cards so everyone can see them, and discard used ones.
- Announce every number you spot in the art — hidden numbers are the core puzzle.
- Use hints rather than guessing blindly; penalties cost more time.

## Common mistakes

- Blue + red is addition only; grey cards combine with anything.
- Do not flip cards just to peek; reveal only when a number points to them.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

# Shadows in Kyoto

> Chinese name: **京都谍影** · Doc #2 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../02_%E4%BA%AC%E9%83%BD%E8%B0%8D%E5%BD%B1_Shadows_in_Kyoto.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/2)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 2 (best: 2) |
| Play time | 15–30 min |
| Age | 10+ |
| Complexity | ●●○○○ Light (2/5) |
| Tags | 2-player, deduction, hidden role, light strategy, talky |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg)
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 中间那摞

## Overview

A two-player bakumatsu spy duel: six face-down agents with hidden values and real or fake intel move with location cards and capture by collision.

## Setup

1. Each side has 6 agents: 3 (real), 3 (fake), 2 (real), 2 (fake), 1 (fake), 0 (real); real intel shows a red dot.
2. Place 5 agents on your back row and 1 in front of the middle space, values facing you.
3. Shuffle the location and tactic decks; each player draws 4 location + 2 tactic cards (6 total).

## How to play

1. Play 1 card, then refill to 6 from either deck.
2. Location cards: move one agent 1 space forward or diagonally forward onto a space of the card's colour (wild cards excepted). No sideways or backward moves.
3. Your agents cannot share a space and cannot enter the enemy HQ unless an ability says so.
4. Conflict: moving onto an enemy agent makes you the attacker. The defender reveals; the attacker states its value truthfully without revealing.
5. Compare: 3 > 2 > 1 > 0, but 0 beats 3. The attacker wins on greater or equal and captures the defender.
6. Tactic cards (e.g. Defence) allow special moves such as stepping sideways or back.

## Game end & scoring

- Win by capturing enough real intel, or by getting a real-intel agent into the enemy HQ (see rulebook for exact conditions).
- Advanced play adds charm and equipment cards.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win by capturing enough real intel, or by getting a real-intel agent into the enemy HQ (see rulebook for exact conditions).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play 1 card, then refill to 6 from either deck.
3. **Setup check:** Each side has 6 agents: 3 (real), 3 (fake), 2 (real), 2 (fake), 1 (fake), 0 (real); real intel shows a red dot.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Location cards only allow moves onto matching colours.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- 0 beats 3 — hide your 0 where the enemy 3 will travel.
- Attackers only announce values, so attacking keeps your real/fake status secret.
- Save Defence-type tactics for when an agent is cornered.

## Common mistakes

- Location cards only allow moves onto matching colours.
- Check the rulebook for what happens when both decks run out.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels; talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

# Northern Branch: Firm with Brownies

> Chinese name: **北方分部：精灵商会** · Doc #3 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../03_%E5%8C%97%E6%96%B9%E5%88%86%E9%83%A8_Northern_Branch_Firm_with_Brownies.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/3)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 2–4 (best: 3) |
| Play time | 30 min |
| Age | 12+ |
| Complexity | ●●●○○ Medium (3/5) |
| Tags | economic, engine, cards, strategy, anime |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg)
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 中间那摞
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 中间那摞

## Overview

A 'straw to mansion' trading engine: turn resources into goods, upgrade to higher goods, then build buildings whose brownies work for you.

## Setup

1. Market of 4 rows: buildings (5 face up), secondary goods (5), primary goods (5, same names stacked), and all resource cards (stacked).
2. Each card shows its value top-left and its required materials top-right.
3. Each player starts with an opening-funds card; the VP cards form a stack.

## How to play

1. Refill the building, secondary and primary rows to 5.
2. Player action (once per turn, choose one): Produce — pay the materials shown on a card to take it; or Trade — exchange cards by value, but only one-for-many or many-for-one.
3. Brownie actions: each building you own can be used once per turn (turn it sideways).
4. End of turn (new in this game): if you hold chickens or pigs, reveal them and take 1 Dung card (max 1 per turn).

## Game end & scoring

- The first player to reach the VP target wins (see rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** The first player to reach the VP target wins (see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Refill the building, secondary and primary rows to 5.
3. **Setup check:** Market of 4 rows: buildings (5 face up), secondary goods (5), primary goods (5, same names stacked), and all resource cards (stacked).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Hand limit 7 is absolute; you cannot even temporarily hold 8.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Dung is the only resource you get without spending an action — use it for compost or fuel goods.
- Buildings early pay off over many turns.
- Plan around the hard hand limit of 7.

## Common mistakes

- Hand limit 7 is absolute; you cannot even temporarily hold 8.
- The market refills only at the start of each player's turn.
- Not compatible with the previous game's cards.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** most board gamers
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

# Dead Man's Draw

> Chinese name: **亡者神抽** · Doc #4 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../04_%E4%BA%A1%E8%80%85%E7%A5%9E%E6%8A%BD_Dead_Mans_Draw.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/4)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 2–4 (best: 2) |
| Play time | 15–20 min |
| Age | 13+ |
| Complexity | ●○○○○ Very light (1/5) |
| Tags | push-your-luck, cards, family, light strategy |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg)
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 中间那摞

## Overview

A pirate push-your-luck card game: keep flipping cards to win more loot, but a duplicate suit busts everything.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck; each player has an empty bank area.

## How to play

1. Flip cards one by one into the play area; each card's suit ability triggers when it appears.
2. Stop at any time to bank all cards in the play area.
3. If you flip a suit already in the play area, you bust and discard everything flipped this turn.

## Game end & scoring

- When the deck runs out, score the highest card of each suit in your bank; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When the deck runs out, score the highest card of each suit in your bank; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Flip cards one by one into the play area; each card's suit ability triggers when it appears.
3. **Setup check:** Shuffle the deck; each player has an empty bank area.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Only the top card of each suit in your bank scores.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Learn the suit abilities — some steal, some let you peek or draw again.
- The more suits are out, the riskier the next flip.
- Stealing from opponents' high cards can swing the game.

## Common mistakes

- Only the top card of each suit in your bank scores.
- Busting discards everything from this turn.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

# Mindbug: First Contact

> Chinese name: **心灵虫** · Doc #5 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../05_%E5%BF%83%E7%81%B5%E8%99%AB_Mindbug.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/5)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 2 (best: 2) |
| Play time | 15–20 min |
| Age | 8+ |
| Complexity | ●●○○○ Light (2/5) |
| Tags | 2-player, cards, combat, light strategy |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg)
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 中间那摞底部

## Overview

A lightweight duel card game: play creatures to attack, but each player has two Mindbugs to steal a creature the moment the opponent plays it.

## Setup

1. Each player starts with 3 life, 5 cards in hand, a 5-card draw pile and 2 Mindbug cards.

## How to play

1. On your turn either play a creature or attack with one creature.
2. When you play a creature, your opponent may use a Mindbug to take control of it (and you take another turn).
3. When attacking, the defender may block with a creature; higher power wins, ties destroy both.
4. Unblocked attacks cost the defender 1 life. Draw back to 5 cards while your draw pile lasts.

## Game end & scoring

- Reduce your opponent to 0 life, or win if the opponent cannot act.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Reduce your opponent to 0 life, or win if the opponent cannot act.
2. **What you do on a turn:** On your turn either play a creature or attack with one creature.
3. **Setup check:** Each player starts with 3 life, 5 cards in hand, a 5-card draw pile and 2 Mindbug cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Using a Mindbug gives the opponent an extra turn.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Bait your opponent's Mindbugs with strong but not crucial creatures.
- Track how many Mindbugs each side has left.
- Keywords (Poisonous, Tough, Frenzy, Sneaky, Hunter) decide most fights.

## Common mistakes

- Using a Mindbug gives the opponent an extra turn.
- Poisonous kills any creature it battles.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** couples / head-to-head duels; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

# The Fart Prophet

> Chinese name: **屁者先知** · Doc #6 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../06_%E5%B1%81%E8%80%85%E5%85%88%E7%9F%A5.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/6)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 5–10 (best: 7-8) |
| Play time | 15–45 min |
| Age | 12+ |
| Complexity | ●●○○○ Light (2/5) |
| Tags | party, teams, deduction, hidden role, funny |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg)
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 右侧

## Overview

A comedic team deduction game in an elevator: a hidden Farter team knows which floors stink; everyone holds breath, accuses and votes over 8 floors.

## Setup

1. Take identity and environment cards for the player count; each player gets a speaking-order card and a secret identity.
2. Each player draws 2 character cards and keeps 1; set health to 4.
3. A dealer lays out 8 face-down environment cards (one per floor); the Farter team sees which floors have farts.

## How to play

1. Decide whether to play an action card face down (not playing means no action this round).
2. Reveal this floor's environment card and resolve actions in order (protect, hold breath, accuse…).
3. On fart floors: affected players lose health, then everyone speaks and points at a suspect on three; most votes is eliminated regardless of health.
4. Ties revote between the tied players; another tie means nobody leaves.
5. Once 2+ players are out, an eliminated player may secretly check identities (see rulebook).

## Game end & scoring

- A team wins immediately when the other team is fully eliminated.
- After 8 floors, the team with more survivors wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** A team wins immediately when the other team is fully eliminated.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Decide whether to play an action card face down (not playing means no action this round).
3. **Setup check:** Take identity and environment cards for the player count; each player gets a speaking-order card and a secret identity.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Players at 0 health are out and cannot speak.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Farters: reacting perfectly every time gives you away.
- Good players: track who always seems prepared for fart floors.
- Advanced supernatural identities add twists.

## Common mistakes

- Players at 0 health are out and cannot speak.
- Vote ties are re-voted once.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

# Take Time

> Chinese name: **Take Time** · Doc #7 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../07_Take_Time.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/7)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 2–4 (best: 3) |
| Play time | 30–45 min |
| Age | 10+ |
| Complexity | ●●●○○ Medium (3/5) |
| Tags | co-op, numbers, puzzle-solving, cards, silent |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2 / A3
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg)
- Original note (Chinese): A2 货架（照片 155038 上层，155036 下层） → 右侧
- Original note (Chinese): A3 货架 → Take Time（彩色盒）

## Overview

A silent cooperative campaign puzzle (Libellud): place 12 face-down sun/moon number cards around the 6 sectors of a clock so sector sums never decrease clockwise and never exceed 24.

## Setup

1. Components: 24 number cards (12 white sun cards 1–12, 12 black moon cards 1–12), clock hand, 3 reminder and 3 bonus tokens, 10 chapter envelopes of 4 clocks each (40 levels), a Regrets envelope and a Rebirth envelope.
2. Reveal the level's clock, shuffle sun and moon cards and deal 12 cards among players (distribution by player count per rulebook); place reminder/bonus tokens as instructed.

## How to play

1. Discussion phase: read the level's rules and discuss strategy freely BEFORE looking at cards — no codes and no revealing values.
2. Placement phase (silence): pick up your cards and place them face down next to the sectors until all 12 are placed. Tokens may be used under their conditions.
3. General rules: every sector gets at least 1 card; starting from the marked sector and going clockwise, sector sums must be increasing OR EQUAL; no sector sum may exceed 24.
4. Resolution: flip clockwise from the hand and check every rule. Success advances; failure can be retried or the level placed in the Regrets envelope.

## Game end & scoring

- Each chapter has 4 levels (10 chapters, 40 levels); new symbols and rules are added as you progress.
- After all levels, open the Rebirth envelope for the final challenge.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Each chapter has 4 levels (10 chapters, 40 levels); new symbols and rules are added as you progress.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Discussion phase: read the level's rules and discuss strategy freely BEFORE looking at cards — no codes and no revealing values.
3. **Setup check:** Components: 24 number cards (12 white sun cards 1–12, 12 black moon cards 1–12), clock hand, 3 reminder and 3 bonus tokens, 10 chapter envelopes of 4 clocks each (40 levels), a Regrets envelope and a Rebirth envelope.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Equal sums are allowed; decreasing is not.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Agree an overall shape (small numbers early, big later) and who should play first.
- Hesitation and play order carry information.
- Debriefing after a failure is part of the fun.

## Common mistakes

- Equal sums are allowed; decreasing is not.
- No talking once cards are picked up.
- Each sector needs at least one card.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** friends who prefer working together
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

# Coda (Da Vinci Code)

> Chinese name: **达芬奇密码** · Doc #8 · [中文原文 / Chinese doc](../08_%E8%BE%BE%E8%8A%AC%E5%A5%87%E5%AF%86%E7%A0%81_Da_Vinci_Code.md) · [Open in the web app](https://codinggeoff.github.io/boardgame-shelf-rules/#doc/8)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Players | 2–4 (best: 2-3) |
| Play time | 15 min |
| Age | 8+ |
| Complexity | ●○○○○ Very light (1/5) |
| Tags | deduction, numbers, family, classic |

## Where is it on the shelf?

- **Shelf code:** A2
- **Photos:** [155036](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155036.jpg), [155038](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155038.jpg), [155049](../../photos/web/IMG20261127155049.jpg)

- Original note (Chinese): 照片 155049 上层与左侧 → 左边缘

## Overview

Arrange black and white number tiles in ascending order as your code, then guess opponents' tiles; keep guessing while you are right.

## Setup

5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

- 13s are deadly — swap them.
- Call only when confident.
- Multiple identical cards may be replaced together (per rules).

## Common mistakes

- The caller cannot act after calling.

## Game end & scoring

- Legacy changes are permanent.

## Overview

Two submarines in real time: the captain calls headings, the radio operator tracks the enemy, the first mate charges systems, the engineer handles breakdowns.

## Setup

1. Two teams behind a screen, same map; four roles each.

## How to play

1. Captain announces moves; engineer marks breakdowns; first mate charges systems.
2. Radio operator traces enemy moves on a transparent sheet.
3. Use torpedoes, mines, sonar, drones; surface to repair (revealing your sector).

## Game end & scoring

- Deal 4 damage to the enemy sub to win.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Deal 4 damage to the enemy sub to win.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Captain announces moves; engineer marks breakdowns; first mate charges systems.
3. **Setup check:** Two teams behind a screen, same map; four roles each.
4. **The one rule people forget:** In real time, the captain waits for confirmations.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Use the transparency to eliminate positions.
- Mines block chokepoints.
- Avoid repeated breakdowns on one circuit.

## Common mistakes

- In real time, the captain waits for confirmations.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

## Overview

Draft cards over 3 ages: build resources, military, science, commerce and wonders; trade with neighbours.

## Setup

1. Each player takes a wonder board (A/B side) and 3 coins.

## How to play

1. Pick 1 card and pass the rest (left, right, left).
2. Build it, use it for a wonder stage, or discard for 3 coins.
3. Buy resources from neighbours for 2 coins each.

## Game end & scoring

- Military conflicts after each age; score 7 categories; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Military conflicts after each age; score 7 categories; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Pick 1 card and pass the rest (left, right, left).
3. **Setup check:** Each player takes a wonder board (A/B side) and 3 coins.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Chains build for free.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Hate-draft neighbours' needs.
- Commit to science or ignore it.
- Military only compares neighbours.

## Common mistakes

- Chains build for free.

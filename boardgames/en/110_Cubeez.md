## Overview

Blue Orange real-time puzzle: rotate, flip and swap your 3 wooden cubes to build the funny face shown on a challenge card.

## Setup

1. 12 wooden cubes (4 colours × 3, each face printed with eyes, mouths etc.) and 50 challenge cards.
2. Each player takes 3 cubes of one colour; the youngest starts.

## How to play

1. Reveal a challenge card showing a 3-cube face.
2. Everyone simultaneously turns, flips and swaps their cubes to match.
3. The first to finish puts a hand on the card; if correct they win it.
4. Reveal the next card.

## Game end & scoring

- Play to the target number of cards in the rulebook; most cards wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Play to the target number of cards in the rulebook; most cards wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Reveal a challenge card showing a 3-cube face.
3. **Setup check:** 12 wooden cubes (4 colours × 3, each face printed with eyes, mouths etc.) and 50 challenge cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** A wrong build loses the card (per rulebook).
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Find the eyes first.
- Learn which features share a cube.
- Orientation matters.

## Common mistakes

- A wrong build loses the card (per rulebook).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

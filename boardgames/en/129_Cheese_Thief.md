## Overview

No-moderator 10-minute deduction: everyone secretly rolls a die; on the night count, players whose number is called wake; the thief steals the cheese.

## Setup

1. Roles by count: 4p 1 thief + 3 sleepyheads (2 dice each); 5p 1 thief + 4; 6p adds a scapegoat; 7–8p add more sleepyheads; accomplice limit 1–2.
2. Everyone secretly rolls in their cup; only you know your number.

## How to play

1. Night: everyone closes eyes; someone counts 1 to 6; players whose die shows that number open eyes.
2. A sleepyhead awake alone may peek at another die; the thief steals the cheese on his number and may recruit accomplices.
3. Day: discuss freely — lying is allowed, but you may not reveal cups or roles.
4. Vote simultaneously; the most-voted player reveals.

## Game end & scoring

- Catching the thief wins for the sleepyheads; otherwise the thief side wins (scapegoat rules per rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Catching the thief wins for the sleepyheads; otherwise the thief side wins (scapegoat rules per rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Night: everyone closes eyes; someone counts 1 to 6; players whose die shows that number open eyes.
3. **Setup check:** Roles by count: 4p 1 thief + 3 sleepyheads (2 dice each); 5p 1 thief + 4; 6p adds a scapegoat; 7–8p add more sleepyheads; accomplice limit 1–2.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Never reveal your cup or role card.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Players awake together are each other's witnesses.
- The thief may lie about his number.
- Accomplices deflect.

## Common mistakes

- Never reveal your cup or role card.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

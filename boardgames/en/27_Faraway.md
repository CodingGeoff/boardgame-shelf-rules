## Overview

Play 8 region cards over 8 rounds, then score backwards from the 8th card: later cards 'see' all cards before them.

## Setup

1. Each player draws 3 region cards; reveal a market of regions.
2. Prepare the sanctuary deck.

## How to play

1. Everyone simultaneously plays 1 region card in a row.
2. If its number is higher than your previous card, draw sanctuaries and keep one.
3. In ascending card-number order, players pick a new region from the market.

## Game end & scoring

- Flip cards from the 8th back to the 1st; each card scores using only itself, cards to its right and sanctuaries.
- Highest total wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Flip cards from the 8th back to the 1st; each card scores using only itself, cards to its right and sanctuaries.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Everyone simultaneously plays 1 region card in a row.
3. **Setup check:** Each player draws 3 region cards; reveal a market of regions.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Scoring is in reverse order.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Early cards should provide icons and resources; play quest cards later.
- Low numbers pick earlier; high numbers gain sanctuaries.
- Sanctuaries always count.

## Common mistakes

- Scoring is in reverse order.
- Sanctuaries need a higher number than your previous card.

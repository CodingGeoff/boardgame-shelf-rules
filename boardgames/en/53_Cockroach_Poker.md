## Overview

Pass pest cards face down while claiming a type; the receiver calls true/false or passes it on. Collect 4 of a kind and you lose.

## Setup

1. Deal all 64 cards evenly; remove extras face down.

## How to play

1. Pass a card face down to anyone while naming a pest.
2. Receiver either calls true or false (the wrong party takes the card face up), or peeks and passes it on with a new claim (not to someone who has seen it).

## Game end & scoring

- A player with 4 of the same pest face up, or no cards when they must pass, loses.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** A player with 4 of the same pest face up, or no cards when they must pass, loses.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Pass a card face down to anyone while naming a pest.
3. **Setup check:** Deal all 64 cards evenly; remove extras face down.
4. **The one rule people forget:** A card cannot go to someone who already saw it.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- The truth is often hardest to detect.
- Target players with 3 of a kind.
- Peeking and passing gains information.

## Common mistakes

- A card cannot go to someone who already saw it.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** talkative groups who enjoy acting and bluffing; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** players who dislike conflict or backstabbing

### Worked example (illustrative)

1. Deal all 64 cards.
2. I pass a card to A: 'Scorpion.'
3. A peeks and passes to B: 'Spider.'
4. B says 'false' — correct, so A takes the card face up.
5. A collects a 4th scorpion and loses.

### Scoring example

- Four of a kind (5 with 2p) or no cards to pass loses.

## Overview

Grab the right wooden object: if a card shows an object in its true colour, grab it; otherwise grab the one sharing neither colour nor shape.

## Setup

1. Place the 5 objects in the centre.

## How to play

1. Reveal a card; everyone grabs at once.
2. Rule 1: object in correct colour → grab it.
3. Rule 2: none correct → grab the object whose colour and shape are both absent.

## Game end & scoring

- Most cards wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Most cards wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Reveal a card; everyone grabs at once.
3. **Setup check:** Place the 5 objects in the centre.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Grab only one object.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Classify the card first.
- Wrong grabs give a card back.
- Add expansion rules later.

## Common mistakes

- Grab only one object.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner; fans of speed and excitement
- **Not ideal for:** people who want to think slowly and quietly

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 20–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 30 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Flip a card and grab the right wooden object: if one object appears in its true colour, grab it; otherwise grab the one whose shape and colour are both absent. Correct grabs win the card; wrong grabs cost a card. Most cards wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Learn the five objects and colours.
- **Mid:** Use elimination fast.
- **Late:** Avoid false grabs.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Wrong grab?**  
  A: Give up a won card.
- **Q: What does the sequel add?**  
  A: New objects and rules.

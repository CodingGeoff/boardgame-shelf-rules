## Overview

Fill dog cards with spots by rolling dice through trick cards; if the yard totals more than 7, you bust.

## Setup

1. Each player takes 2 starting dog cards.
2. Reveal trick cards as shared action options; give each player treats.

## How to play

1. Choose one: perform a trick — roll dice as the card says and place dice on matching spots or in the yard; or score a finished dog.
2. If dice in the yard total more than 7, you bust and lose this turn's progress per rules.
3. Spend a treat to reroll.

## Game end & scoring

- First to complete 6 dogs wins (target may change by player count).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First to complete 6 dogs wins (target may change by player count).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Choose one: perform a trick — roll dice as the card says and place dice on matching spots or in the yard; or score a finished dog.
3. **Setup check:** Each player takes 2 starting dog cards.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Bust is more than 7, not exactly 7.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- When the yard is nearly full, pick tricks with fewer dice.
- Treats are your only rerolls — save them.
- Finish small dogs first to build momentum.

## Common mistakes

- Bust is more than 7, not exactly 7.
- Scoring a dog uses your turn.

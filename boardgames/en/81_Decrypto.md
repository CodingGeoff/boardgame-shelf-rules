## Overview

Two teams each have 4 secret keywords and transmit 3-digit codes with clues; the other team tries to intercept.

## Setup

1. Two screens; each team places 4 keyword cards in slots 1–4 visible only to them.

## How to play

1. An encryptor draws a code (e.g. 3-1-4) and gives 3 clues.
2. Their team guesses the code; the opponents try to intercept (not in round 1).
3. Miscommunication earns a white token; interceptions earn black tokens.

## Game end & scoring

- 2 interceptions win; 2 miscommunications lose; tiebreaks per rules after 8 rounds.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** 2 interceptions win; 2 miscommunications lose; tiebreaks per rules after 8 rounds.
2. **What you do on a turn:** An encryptor draws a code (e.g. 3-1-4) and gives 3 clues.
3. **Setup check:** Two screens; each team places 4 keyword cards in slots 1–4 visible only to them.
4. **The one rule people forget:** No interceptions in round 1.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Use inside jokes only your team knows.
- Vary association types.
- Log the opponents' clues per number.

## Common mistakes

- No interceptions in round 1.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

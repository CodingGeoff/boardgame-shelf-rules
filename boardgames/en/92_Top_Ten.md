## Overview

Fully cooperative: each player has a secret number 1–10 and answers a theme to express it; the captain orders the answers.

## Setup

1. Draw theme cards; set unicorn tokens (lives).

## How to play

1. The captain reads a theme; each player gives an answer reflecting their number.
2. The captain orders players from low to high; mistakes cost unicorn tokens.

## Game end & scoring

- Win by completing the theme deck before lives run out.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Win by completing the theme deck before lives run out.
2. **What you do on a turn:** The captain reads a theme; each player gives an answer reflecting their number.
3. **Setup check:** Draw theme cards; set unicorn tokens (lives).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Never say numbers.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Spread answers across the range.
- Exaggerate for 1s and 10s.
- Red themes are adult-oriented.

## Common mistakes

- Never say numbers.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; friends who prefer working together; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 20–30 min; budget about 35 min for a first game.
- Total: about 40 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Fully cooperative. Everyone gets a secret 1–10; the captain reads a theme. Answer in a way that conveys your number; the captain orders everyone. Mistakes cost hearts.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Answer within the theme.
- **Mid:** Calibrate from others.
- **Late:** Captain sorts from extremes.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Can I say my number?**  
  A: No.
- **Q: Does the captain rotate?**  
  A: Yes.

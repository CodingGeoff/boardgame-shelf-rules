## Overview

Pay a chip to pass a card, or take it with the chips on it; runs score only their lowest card; lowest score wins.

## Setup

1. Remove 9 random cards from 3–35; chips: 11 each (3–5p), 9 (6p), 7 (7p).

## How to play

1. Reveal a card; in turn, pay 1 chip to pass or take the card and its chips.
2. The taker reveals the next card.

## Game end & scoring

- Score card values, counting only the lowest card of each run, minus chips; lowest wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Score card values, counting only the lowest card of each run, minus chips; lowest wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Reveal a card; in turn, pay 1 chip to pass or take the card and its chips.
3. **Setup check:** Remove 9 random cards from 3–35; chips: 11 each (3–5p), 9 (6p), 7 (7p).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Each chip is worth −1 point.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Let cards that extend your run circle to collect chips.
- Chips are your lifeline.
- Removed cards may break runs.

## Common mistakes

- Each chip is worth −1 point.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Advanced guide: teaching script, time budget, phase strategy, extra FAQ

> Summarised by the editor from the rules above for teaching and improving; the rulebook wins on conflicts.

### First-game time budget

- Teaching: about 5 min (weight ●).
- Play: 20–20 min; budget about 25 min for a first game.
- Total: about 30 min.

### Teaching script (read it aloud)

> Reveal a number card. On your turn pay 1 chip to say 'No Thanks', or take the card and all chips on it. Runs only count their lowest card. Score = card sum − chips; lowest wins.

### Strategy by phase

- **Early:** Take cards that build runs.
- **Mid:** Let chips accumulate before taking.
- **Late:** Low on chips? Avoid being forced.

### Extra FAQ

- **Q: Out of chips?**  
  A: You must take the card.
- **Q: How many cards are removed?**  
  A: 9, unseen.

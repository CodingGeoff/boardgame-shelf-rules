## Overview

Flip cards one by one; stop when you like. A duplicate number busts; 7 unique numbers gives +15.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the 94-card deck; each round deal 1 card to each player.

## How to play

1. On your turn choose: hit (take another card) or stay.
2. Duplicate number cards bust you for the round.
3. Modifier and action cards: Freeze, Flip Three, Second Chance.

## Game end & scoring

- Flip 7 ends the round instantly with +15.
- First to 200 points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** Flip 7 ends the round instantly with +15.
2. **What you do on a turn:** On your turn choose: hit (take another card) or stay.
3. **Setup check:** Shuffle the 94-card deck; each round deal 1 card to each player.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Number n appears n times (0 once).
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Count cards: many high numbers out lowers the risk.
- Second Chance lets you push.
- Give Flip Three to the leader.

## Common mistakes

- Number n appears n times (0 once).

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

## Overview

A pirate push-your-luck card game: keep flipping cards to win more loot, but a duplicate suit busts everything.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck; each player has an empty bank area.

## How to play

1. Flip cards one by one into the play area; each card's suit ability triggers when it appears.
2. Stop at any time to bank all cards in the play area.
3. If you flip a suit already in the play area, you bust and discard everything flipped this turn.

## Game end & scoring

- When the deck runs out, score the highest card of each suit in your bank; most points wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** When the deck runs out, score the highest card of each suit in your bank; most points wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Flip cards one by one into the play area; each card's suit ability triggers when it appears.
3. **Setup check:** Shuffle the deck; each player has an empty bank area.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Only the top card of each suit in your bank scores.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Learn the suit abilities — some steal, some let you peek or draw again.
- The more suits are out, the riskier the next flip.
- Stealing from opponents' high cards can swing the game.

## Common mistakes

- Only the top card of each suit in your bank scores.
- Busting discards everything from this turn.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** icebreakers; newcomers and grandparents can learn it instantly; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

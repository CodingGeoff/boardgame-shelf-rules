## Overview

Make all your pigs dirty: mud dirties them, barns protect from rain, lightning destroys barns, farmers wash pigs.

## Setup

1. Each player has 3–5 clean pigs (more with fewer players); deal 3 cards each.

## How to play

1. Play 1 card and refill to 3; if unable, discard your hand and redraw.
2. Mud: dirty your pig. Rain: cleans all dirty pigs not in barns. Barn: protects from rain. Lightning: destroys a barn. Lightning rod: protects a barn. Barn lock: blocks farmers. Farmer: washes an opponent's pig.

## Game end & scoring

- First player with all pigs dirty wins.

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** First player with all pigs dirty wins.
2. **What you do on a turn:** Play 1 card and refill to 3; if unable, discard your hand and redraw.
3. **Setup check:** Each player has 3–5 clean pigs (more with fewer players); deal 3 cards each.
4. **The one rule people forget:** Card names vary between editions.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Barn + lock + lightning rod is invulnerable.
- Rain also washes your pigs — check before playing.
- Hold a rain card as a threat.

## Common mistakes

- Card names vary between editions.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** families with young children; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit

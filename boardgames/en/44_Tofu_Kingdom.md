## Overview

The Mochi Prince questions everyone to find the Tofu Princess; some roles must tell the truth, some must lie, some choose.

## Setup

1. The prince closes eyes; others take role cards and show each other (including the middle card).

## How to play

1. Princess and Chef must tell the truth; Queen, Minister and Guard must lie; Spy and Maid may do either.
2. The prince asks each player one question from three allowed formats.
3. After all questions, the prince may ask one more to anyone.

## Game end & scoring

- The prince names the princess; each team scores by its win condition (see rulebook).

## 5-minute teaching order

1. **Goal first:** The prince names the princess; each team scores by its win condition (see rulebook).
2. **What you do on a turn:** Princess and Chef must tell the truth; Queen, Minister and Guard must lie; Spy and Maid may do either.
3. **Setup check:** The prince closes eyes; others take role cards and show each other (including the middle card).
4. **The one rule people forget:** Questions must use the allowed formats.
5. Play one open-handed practice round, then start for real.

## Strategy tips

- Prince: cross-check answers to find contradictions.
- Queen's team: steer suspicion onto wrong people.
- Spies are unpredictable — account for them.

## Common mistakes

- Questions must use the allowed formats.
- Answers must follow each role's truth rule.

## Good for / not for

- **Good for:** big parties; people who love deduction and guessing identities; short gaps — waiting for friends, before or after dinner
- **Not ideal for:** no obvious bad fit
